How TKVK and Taj HR Services fit together

TKVK — Taj Kaushal Vikas Kendra — is a skill training and trade testing centre in New Delhi. Taj HR Services is the licensed recruiting agent. TKVK tests and prepares the worker; Taj HR Services recruits and deploys him under Government of India licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025.

That split matters when you are checking an agency. A testing centre that claimed the licence would be misrepresenting itself, and a recruiting agent that claimed to test every man practically would be worth asking to prove it. Here you can check both halves separately, and we would rather you did.

Licence and company questions

Licence and authorisation

Are you a licensed recruitment agency or manpower supplier?

TKVK is a skill training and trade testing centre in New Delhi, not the licence holder. Recruitment, emigration clearance and deployment run through Taj HR Services, which holds the Government of India recruiting agent licence. We test and prepare the men; the licensed agency recruits and deploys them.

What is your recruitment licence number?

The licence number is B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025 and it is issued to Taj HR Services. TKVK does not hold a recruitment licence of its own and does not present itself as holding one. Any agency that blurs that line is worth a second look.

Which authority issued your licence?

India's Ministry of External Affairs, through the office of the Protector General of Emigrants. It is the only authority in India that licenses recruiting agents for overseas employment, and a licence from anyone else is not a recruitment licence.

How can we verify your licence?

Check the number against the registered recruiting agent list on the Government of India eMigrate portal, or ask the Protector of Emigrants office in Delhi directly. We would rather you verified it than took our word for it.

Are you authorised to recruit workers from India?

Recruitment and emigration clearance are carried out by Taj HR Services under its MEA licence. TKVK's authorised part of the work is the training, trade testing and document preparation that happens before that. The two halves together are what reaches your site.

The markets we serve

Which Gulf countries do you serve?

All six GCC states: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. Each has its own page setting out the trades, the documents and the entry requirements for that market.

Do you recruit for Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain?

Yes, all six. The trades we test are the same, but the paperwork, the work-permit route and the midday summer rules differ by country, so each country is handled on its own terms rather than as one Gulf package.

Do you have experience with GCC recruitment?

The GCC is the only market we work for. The trade tests, the document sequence and the candidate briefing are all built around GCC entry requirements and nothing else, which is a narrower claim than being everywhere and a more useful one.

Company, office and references

How many years have you been in recruitment?

Ask us and we will tell you plainly, with the registration and licence papers to match. We do not print a number of years on a web page without the document that proves it beside it.

Can you share your company registration and office address?

The office and testing centre are at 18-B, 1st Floor, Money Top, Navjivan Buddh Vihar, Taimoor Nagar, New Friends Colony, New Delhi 110025. Registration and licence documents are sent on request to a company email, not published here.

Do you have a physical office in India?

Yes, in New Delhi, and the trade testing takes place there rather than at a third-party yard. That matters, because a centre you can walk into is a centre where you can watch a candidate work before you select him.

Can we visit your office or conduct a video call?

Both. Employers are welcome at the New Delhi centre, and if travel is not practical we run the same session over video with the candidate at the bench and the work in frame. Tell us the trade in advance so the material is set up.

Can you share client references?

Not on a public page. Employer names are not ours to publish without their written permission. Ask during the enquiry and we will tell you exactly what we are free to share and what we are not.

Can you share examples of similar projects?

We do not publish case studies we cannot evidence, and inventing one would be the easiest thing on this page to do. Tell us the trade, the country and the number of men and we will say plainly whether we have handled that kind of requirement.

Do you have experience with our industry?

Tell us which one. We work for construction, industrial and plant maintenance, infrastructure, logistics and warehousing, facility management, hospitality and retail. If your sector is outside that list we will say so instead of stretching to fit it.
