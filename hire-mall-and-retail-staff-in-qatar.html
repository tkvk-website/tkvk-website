Why retail support staff are assessed before you interview them

Qatar's retail estate was built fast and built large. Doha Festival City, Place Vendôme and Mall of Qatar are leisure destinations with retail inside them, which means the support job runs alongside cinemas, indoor attractions and events rather than around a quiet shopping day.

The labour pool is small and mobile, so retention is the real cost. A chain that replaces its stockroom team twice a year pays for the same recruitment three times. We prepare men for the shift pattern and the accommodation they will actually get, which is the single biggest predictor of whether they are still there in a year.

On paper, Qatar does most of the work before departure. Contract attestation and the Qatar Visa Centre biometrics and medical happen in India, so a file that is wrong at our end becomes a rejection at the QVC rather than a problem you discover at the airport.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What we assess

  1. Back-of-house in a leisure destination

    Service corridors shared with cinemas, attractions and event spaces, where a delivery route is blocked for reasons that have nothing to do with retail.

  2. Receiving and put-away

    Checking a delivery against the note, rejecting damage before it is signed for, and getting it off the corridor fast.

  3. Event and weekend surge

    Malls that fill for an event rather than a sale, and the replenishment discipline that keeps shelves full through it.

  4. Scanner and stock system workflow

    Scanning discipline and flagging a mismatch rather than letting it ride. Tell us your system and we brief the workflow.

  5. Conduct and presentation

    Uniform, grooming and bearing in a building where the customer is there for the afternoon, not for one purchase.

What we require before we place anyone

Mall work is public work. A man on a shop floor or in a food court is seen by your customers all day, so we assess conduct the way we assess the task: how he handles being watched, being interrupted, being told he is wrong by a shopper, and being asked something he does not know the answer to. We place men only, and we place them only where the wage, the working hours and the weekly rest day, the accommodation and the shift pattern are set out in writing before he signs. Retail runs on split shifts and late closings more than almost any other sector, and a man who was not told that before he left India is a man who leaves within a year.

Papers and what we will not promise

This category carries no trade certificate and we do not create one. You receive the assessment, an honest statement of prior experience, and full medical and document status. Passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared alongside contract attestation so the file clears the Qatar Visa Centre first time rather than coming back.

What retailers ask us

What goes wrong at the Qatar Visa Centre?

Almost always the file, not the man — a name that does not match the passport, a medical that has expired, or an attestation that was never done. We build the file to clear it first time, and we tell you if something in your paperwork will not.

Do you supply women for retail?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can you supply for a new mall opening?

Tell us the number and the opening date. We give you a schedule built on your visa quota and the QVC timeline, not on what you would prefer to hear.

What keeps these workers from leaving after a year?

Accommodation, the shift pattern and whether the wage matched what he was told. We put all three in writing before he signs, which is not a favour to him so much as protection for your recruitment budget.
