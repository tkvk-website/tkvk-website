The Gulf licence, before you commit

No GCC country lets a man run a forklift on an Indian certificate. Each issues its own licence after local training and testing, and an Indian licence in the same category usually shortens that training rather than replacing it. In Saudi Arabia the forklift licence falls within the heavy-vehicle category, with a minimum age of 21; in the UAE a light forklift licence is issued by the RTA, with a minimum age of 20, and a valid foreign licence can cut the required training hours. We test the man here, send his Indian papers verified, and state in writing that the local licence is arranged on your side.

What employers ask about hiring forklift operators from India

Will his Indian forklift licence work in the Gulf?

No. Each GCC country issues its own after local testing. An Indian licence usually shortens local training; it does not replace it.

Can he run a reach truck, not just a counterbalance?

Only if he has real hours on one, and the profile says so. A counterbalance man in a narrow aisle is how racking gets damaged.

Can one man cover forklift and store work?

Many can, and it is assessed when you say the job needs it.

Can you supply in bulk for a new distribution centre?

Tell us the number and go-live date. The schedule is built on your visa quota and attestation, and we say plainly if the date will not hold.

Do you supply women operators?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only and says so at the start.

Can our manager test them before selection?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video, on the truck.
