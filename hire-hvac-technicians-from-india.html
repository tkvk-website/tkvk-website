Refrigerant experience, man by man

Gulf buildings often run older R-22 equipment next to newer R-410A and R-32 units. Moving from R-22 to the newer gases means higher pressures, POE instead of mineral oil — so moisture and contamination matter more — and, with R-32, a mildly flammable (A2L) gas that changes how a man brazes, recovers and stores it. Each profile records which of these refrigerants the technician has genuine hands-on time with, and says plainly where he has none.

What employers ask about hiring HVAC technicians from India

Do you supply chiller technicians, or only split and package men?

Both, but never interchangeably. Ask for central plant and we send men who have genuinely worked it; unitary-only experience is stated as such.

Can they handle a BMS-controlled system?

Tell us the system. A few men have real BMS exposure and most have none, and we say which. Basic controls are tested for everyone.

How do you judge whether he can actually diagnose?

We give him a faulty unit and watch his method. The technician who checks systematically is the one who closes a callback on the first visit.

Can you supply a technician with a helper, or a team with a supervisor?

Yes. Tell us the workload; on maintenance rounds a technician-and-helper pair is often the cheaper mix.

Do they understand coastal corrosion and humidity?

It is assessed for coastal postings, and we say when a man has only worked dry inland conditions.

Do you supply women technicians?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only, and says so at the start rather than accept the requirement.
