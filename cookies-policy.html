Cookies policy

Most sites that publish a page like this are explaining which trackers they run. This one is explaining that it does not run any. The short version: TKVK sets no cookies of its own on this website, and there is nothing here that follows you between sites.

What this site does not do

It sets no cookies of its own. It stores nothing in your browser — no local storage, no session storage. It runs no analytics, so we cannot see which pages you personally visited. It carries no advertising or remarketing tags, no Meta pixel, no Google Ads tag, and no embedded social widgets that would report your visit back to a social network.

We are not claiming a privacy feature we did not build. The site is a set of plain HTML pages, and tracking was simply never added to it.

The only scripts that run

There are two, and both are served from this domain.

One handles the enquiry form. It does nothing at all until you press send, at which point it posts what you typed to the mailbox that receives enquiries. It does not save a draft, remember you, or run in the background.

The other is a few lines that print the current year in the footer and open and close the menu on a phone.

What the page loads from somewhere else

Three things, and it is worth being precise about when each one happens.

Typefaces, on every page. The fonts come from Google Fonts. Google's servers therefore see the request, which means your IP address and browser. Google states that it does not set cookies for the Fonts service, but the request to Google is real and we would rather name it than leave it out.

The enquiry endpoint, only when you submit. Your message goes to our own mailer so it can be emailed to us. Nothing is sent before you press the button.

WhatsApp, only if you tap it. The WhatsApp buttons are ordinary links. Tapping one takes you to WhatsApp with a message ready to send, and from that point WhatsApp's own terms apply, not ours.

Nothing else on this site is fetched from a third party.

Security and hosting

The site is served through a content delivery network, which is standard for keeping a site fast and resisting attacks. A network of this kind can issue a short-lived technical cookie if it needs to screen unusual traffic. When we checked a normal visit, no such cookie was present. Server logs may record your IP address and browser for security — that is a log, not a cookie, and it is not used to build a profile of you.

Why there is no cookie banner

Consent banners exist so you can refuse tracking that would otherwise happen. There is no such tracking here, so a banner would ask you to approve nothing and would only get in the way of reading the page. If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential tracking, we will ask you first and this page will say so.

Controlling cookies yourself

Every browser lets you see, block and delete cookies in its settings, usually under Privacy. Because this site does not rely on cookies, blocking them will not break anything here. If your browser shows cookies on this domain, check your extensions — extensions can set cookies on sites you visit, and those are outside our control.

Changes to this page

If what the site loads changes, this page changes with it. The date at the foot of the page is when it was last reviewed.
