Hire accommodation and building helpers from India for the Gulf

Cleaners and helpers from India for residential compounds, apartment towers and staff accommodation, prepared at our New Delhi centre on a cleaning routine they must keep with nobody watching, waste handling, and conduct around the people who live there. We supply building helpers to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the building and who lives there

Accommodation work is judged by residents, not by a supervisor. These details decide the shortlist.

Type of accommodation

Family compounds, apartment towers, worker housing or institutional blocks.

Scale

A forty-storey tower, a camp of hundreds, or one small building.

Duties

Cleaning only, or waste, grounds and light maintenance as well.

Shifts

Night or split shifts in accommodation blocks.

Inspections

Whether the site is inspected against enforced standards.

Team shape

Cleaners, a supervisor, and a helper for waste and stores.

What the preparation covers

Method and chemicalsCorrect dilution, colour-coded cloths and mops, and never mixing products.
Routine without supervisionWe set a routine, step back, and check whether the signed sheet matches what was done.
Conduct around residentsKnocking, not entering a unit alone, privacy with belongings, and staying calm with a rude resident.
WasteBagging, bin rooms and chutes, and never leaving a bag in a corridor for later.
Reporting faultsReporting a leak or a broken light instead of walking past it.

What employers ask about hiring accommodation and building helpers from India

Will they keep a schedule without a supervisor on every floor?

That is exactly what we set up and observe, and we report what we saw rather than promise it.

Can they handle light maintenance too?

Say so and we assess it. Reporting a fault instead of ignoring it is tested separately from changing a bulb.

Our compound houses families. Does conduct matter more?

Yes, and it is a graded item. The pre-departure briefing covers it in detail.

Waste is our biggest complaint. Do you test it?

Yes. Waste handling is part of the preparation and reported per man.

Can you supply a full accommodation team?

Tell us the team shape and roles are assessed separately, not six identical men.

We need one or two men only. Is that worth doing?

Yes. Small contracts feel a bad hire immediately, which is why testing matters.

Tell us the accommodation and building helpers you need

Type of accommodation, duties, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
