“Tested” means nothing until you know what was tested

Every agency says its workers are tested. Very few will tell you what the test was. This page sets out exactly what we do, so you can judge whether it is worth anything.

Why it is a separate job

A recruitment agency is paid to fill a headcount. Testing slows that down and sometimes shrinks the batch, so in practice it gets skipped: a certificate is glanced at, a man is put on a flight, and whether he can do the work becomes your problem four thousand kilometres away.

TKVK exists so the testing is somebody’s actual job rather than an optional step in someone else’s. We are not paid more for passing a man than for failing him.

How a test runs

  1. Your standard sets the test

    Before anything, we ask what the work actually is: the codes or standards on your project, the equipment your site uses, the positions and materials involved. A man tested on 6G pipe is not a man tested on flat plate, and we will not blur that to make a batch look better.

  2. The man does the work

    The welder welds. The electrician wires a board and meggers it. The fitter works from an isometric. The operator runs the machine. The cook cooks on the line. Nothing is assessed from a conversation.

  3. Someone who knows the trade judges it

    Finish, method, speed and safety habits — the things a foreman notices in the first hour, assessed by someone competent in that trade rather than by a recruiter with a checklist.

  4. The result is recorded, pass or fail

    The assessment states where the man is strong and where he is weak. A failure is written down and he is retested after the gap is closed. We do not have a category called “probably fine”.

  5. You see the evidence before you interview

    Photographs or video of the test piece, the assessment sheet, and the trade papers — sent before an interview is scheduled, so your technical man can screen without travelling anywhere.

  6. Then you test him yourself, if you want

    In person at our New Delhi centre, or over video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop, the material and the instruments. You are never asked to take our word for it.

What the test does not cover

Being straight about the limits is part of the point.

It is not a certification body

We assess and we record. We do not issue third-party qualifications, and we never present our assessment as one. Where a man holds a real certificate, that goes with the profile as well, described for what it actually proves.

It cannot predict conduct

A test measures skill. It does not tell you how a man will behave in month eight. What it can do is make sure he understood his contract before he signed, which is where most early departures come from.

Some trades test better than others

A weld can be photographed; a housekeeping standard cannot. For service and facility roles we are testing method, hygiene and conduct, and we say that plainly rather than dressing it up as a technical assessment.

What employers ask about the testing

Can we run the test ourselves?

Yes, and we encourage it. Send your technical representative to New Delhi or run it over video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop, the material and the instruments. Our test exists to save your time, not to replace your judgement.

What do we receive before the interview?

The assessment sheet, photographs or video of the test piece where the trade allows it, and the man’s trade and experience papers described for exactly what they prove.

What happens to a man who fails?

He is told what to fix and retested later. He does not quietly reappear on your shortlist, and we do not pad a batch with men who did not pass.

Do you test to a specific code or standard?

Tell us the code, position or standard your project works to and we test against it. We will say so plainly if it is something we cannot test properly rather than approximating it.

Is a certificate not enough?

A certificate tells you what a man studied. It does not tell you whether his weld passes visual inspection, whether he megger-tests before energising, or whether he checks a machine before starting it. Those are the things that decide your first month.
