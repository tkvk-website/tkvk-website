Why household staff are tested before they reach your home

Qatar sets out domestic employment in law — contract, hours and rest day are written obligations, not courtesies. That protects both sides and it removes the usual argument about what was agreed.

What the law cannot tell you is whether the cook can cook. Households here often entertain, and a man who manages a family of four is not automatically a man who can put out food for thirty at short notice.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What we assess

  1. A cooking test, including volume

    Your household's cuisine, cooked and photographed. Where entertaining is part of the job we test cooking for numbers as well, because that is a different skill from daily family meals.

  2. Service and discretion with guests

    Majlis service, handling guests, and understanding that nothing from inside the house is discussed outside it. Assessed as behaviour during preparation.

  3. Family driver assessment where included

    Road test, licence class verified, school-run and passenger conduct. Doha traffic is assessed honestly rather than assumed.

  4. Understanding of hours and rest day

    That the contract sets hours and a weekly rest day, and that these are real. A man who expects to be worked around the clock, and a household that expects to, both create the same problem.

What we require before we place anyone

We place men only in household roles, and we place them only where four things are in the written contract: the wage and how it is paid, the working hours and the weekly rest day, the accommodation and food, and that the worker keeps his own passport. These are not requests. A household that will not put them in writing is one we decline, because the alternative is a man stranded four thousand kilometres from home and a problem that eventually becomes yours too.

Papers and what we will not promise

Domestic employment in Qatar is governed by its own law and the employment documents for this category need attestation by the Indian Mission. Every man clears an approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance where his passport requires it. We confirm the current position for your case rather than working from a template.

Household work carries no trade certificate, so what you receive is the assessment itself — what he cooked, how he drove, what he completed unsupervised — along with the work history we could verify and a plain note where we could not.

What households ask us

Do you supply housemaids?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only — cooks, houseboys, family drivers and gardeners. We say so before taking a requirement rather than after.

We entertain often. Can he cook for thirty?

Tell us and we test it. Cooking for numbers is a separate assessment from family meals and plenty of competent household cooks cannot do it.

Can he serve as well as cook?

Say so and we assess service too — laying, serving, clearing and conduct with guests. A cook who is awkward in front of guests is a different hire from one who is not.

What do you require from the household?

Wage and how it is paid, hours and the weekly rest day, accommodation and food, and that the man keeps his passport — all in writing. We do not proceed without them.

Tell us what your household needs

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Qatar.
