Tell us the driving, not just the licence class

Gulf heavy driving ranges from long highway runs to short, dense yard work. These details decide the shortlist.

Vehicle class

Articulated, tipper, mixer, tanker or bus.

Long-haul or short-run

Hours of highway with fatigue risk, or many site entries and reversing moves a day.

Night running

If convoys or night shifts are part of the job, it is assessed and men are told before they sign.

Hazardous loads

Tanker and hazardous work only for men genuinely ready for it.

Cross-border runs

Runs across the causeway need their own papers for driver, vehicle and load.

Loading duties

Whether he also loads and secures; that part of the day is exposed to summer midday rules.

What we check when a heavy driver is assessed

Road drive in his classHe drives the class he claims. If he claims trailer and drives like a rigid-truck man, that is written down.
Driving under loadBraking distance with weight, gear and speed choice, and how he drives after two hours, not ten minutes.
Pre-trip checkTyres, coupling, load security, lights, brakes and fluids — and whether he really checks or performs it for the assessor.
Yard workReversing, banksman signals and manoeuvring, where most damage happens on short runs.
Licence and yearsClass and validity checked, years held stated, and claims we could not verify marked as such.

Heavy drivers for each Gulf country

In all six countries an Indian heavy licence is not automatically valid; conversion or a local test comes after arrival and before the first trip. Summer midday rules reach loading and securing more than time in an air-conditioned cab.

Licences and papers

Indian heavy licences are verified for class, validity and years held, and previous employer letters or Gulf visa pages are used to evidence experience rather than taking it on trust. An Indian licence is not a Gulf licence in any of the six countries; the local licence or test is arranged on your side after arrival. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. Recruitment and deployment are carried out by Taj HR Services under Government of India licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025.

What employers ask about hiring heavy drivers from India

Will their Indian licence work in the Gulf?

Not automatically, in any of the six countries. Conversion or a local test is arranged after arrival, so build that gap into your start date.

Do you supply tanker and hazardous goods drivers?

Only men genuinely ready for it. We will not upgrade a flatbed driver on paper to fill a batch.

Can you assess for night driving and long runs?

Yes, when you tell us it is part of the job, and men are told the real schedule before they sign.

How do you verify years of experience?

Licence issue date, previous employer letters and the road drive itself. Claims we could not verify are marked as unverified.

Do you have drivers with Gulf experience?

Some, evidenced by earlier visa pages and employer letters. A first-time traveller is never described as experienced.

Can our transport manager test them?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video.

Tell us the heavy drivers you need

Vehicle class, type of running, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
