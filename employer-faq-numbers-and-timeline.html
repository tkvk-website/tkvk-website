Numbers, timelines and mobilisation

Sixteen questions about scale and speed. The honest headline: testing is days, and everything after it runs at the speed of your permit and the document queue — which is why we will not quote a date before seeing your paperwork.

Where the time actually goes

Sourcing, testing and putting a shortlist in front of you is the fast stage. What takes the time is the permit on your side, the attestation, the approved medical and emigration clearance — and those have queues nobody can shorten by asking.

So we plan backwards from your date and tell you at the start whether it holds. If it does not, you hear that at the beginning, when you can still change the plan, rather than three weeks before the date when you cannot.

Numbers, timeline and mobilisation questions

How many workers

What is the minimum number of workers you can recruit?

There is no fixed floor. Small requirements of three or four men are normal for Bahrain and Oman and we take them. What changes with small numbers is the economics of testing and mobilisation, and we will say so plainly rather than quietly cut corners.

Can you supply 10 workers?

Yes, and ten is a comfortable size — enough to test as a batch and interview in one session. Tell us the trade mix and the start date and you will get a realistic timeline rather than an optimistic one.

Can you supply 50 workers?

Yes. At fifty the constraint stops being candidates and becomes paperwork — medicals, police clearances and emigration clearance all have queues. That is why we sequence documents from day one instead of at the end.

Can you supply 100 workers or more?

Tell us the trade, the country and the date before we answer that, because the honest answer depends on all three. For a single common trade with a reasonable window, yes. For a hundred specialists in six weeks, we would rather say no than take the order and fail you.

Timelines

How quickly can you mobilise workers?

Testing and shortlisting are the fast part — usually days. Mobilisation speed is decided by your side: the demand letter, the attestation, the entry permit and the medical queue. Send us your document position and you will get a date range, not a slogan.

What is the expected recruitment timeline?

Sourcing, testing and a shortlist in front of you is the quickest stage. Everything after it runs at the speed of documents. We give a range once we have seen your demand letter and attestation position, and we do not give one before.

What is the expected visa-processing timeline?

That is set by your country's authorities, not by us — Qiwa in Saudi Arabia, MOHRE in the UAE, and the equivalent labour and immigration systems in Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. We track it and tell you where it is stuck; we cannot speed it up and will not pretend to.

What is the expected deployment timeline?

Deployment follows the entry permit. Once the permit is issued, emigration clearance, ticketing and departure are the remaining steps and they are the ones we control. Ask for the date range in writing and you will get it in writing.

Can you handle urgent recruitment?

Often, yes, and the honest limit is the one we do not control. We will not promise an arrival date that depends on a permit you have not applied for. Tell us the deadline early and we will tell you immediately whether it is reachable.

Mobilisation and departure

Can you send workers in batches?

Yes, and for large requirements batches are usually better. A first batch on site lets your supervisors see the standard and correct the brief before the rest travel, which is cheaper than correcting fifty men at once.

Can you mobilise workers for a specific project start date?

We plan backwards from your date and tell you at the start whether it holds. If it does not, you hear that at the beginning when you can still change the plan, not three weeks before the date when you cannot.

Can you arrange group departures?

Yes, and for batches of any size we prefer it. Men travelling together arrive together, which is simpler for your airport pickup and far better for the men themselves on a first journey.

Can you arrange air tickets?

Yes, ticketing and group departures are arranged as part of mobilisation. Who carries the ticket cost is a commercial term agreed in your service agreement, and it is stated there in writing before anyone travels.

Can you arrange airport pickup?

Arrival pickup is on your side of the line — it needs someone with a vehicle in your city. We give you the flight details and the men's contact numbers in advance so the pickup is arranged before they land, not after.

Can you arrange pre-departure orientation?

Yes, and it is one of the more useful things we do. The man is briefed on the employer, the site, the accommodation, the climate, the working hours and the midday summer rules before he travels, so he arrives knowing what he agreed to.

Can you coordinate worker joining dates?

Yes. Give us the joining dates you need and we will sequence departures against them, including staggering arrivals where your accommodation or induction capacity cannot take everyone on the same day.
