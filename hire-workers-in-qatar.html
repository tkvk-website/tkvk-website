Why testing in India matters for Qatar

Qatar pushes most of the formalities to the India side, which is an advantage if the preparation is good and a delay if it is not. Everything we do is aimed at the worker clearing the Qatar Visa Centre first time.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into Qatar works

  1. Qatar Visa Centre, in India

    Medical examination, biometric enrolment and contract signing are completed at a Qatar Visa Centre before the worker flies. A document error shows up here, not at your gate — provided the file was built properly.

  2. Contract terms fixed before travel

    Because the worker signs in India, the salary, allowances, hours and accommodation you state are what he sees and accepts. Vague terms create disputes later; specific ones do not.

  3. Statutory wage and allowances

    Qatar applies a statutory minimum wage, with food and accommodation allowances payable where the employer does not provide them. Tell us which you provide and we will quote candidates accordingly.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    Qatar is ECR-notified. ECR passport holders need Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure, handled by Taj HR Services as the licensed agent.

What Qatari employers ask us

Does the Qatar Visa Centre process slow things down?

It front-loads the work rather than adding to it. Files that were prepared carelessly fail there; files prepared properly move through. We build the file for that checkpoint from day one.

Do workers understand the contract they sign at QVC?

They do by the time they reach it. Terms are explained in the worker’s own language during the pre-departure briefing, before he signs anything.

Can you handle bulk mobilisation?

Yes, with a realistic schedule rather than a promised one. We tell you the constraint — usually medical slots or attestation — instead of discovering it halfway.

Will we see test evidence before interviewing?

Yes. Assessment sheets and test-piece evidence are sent with the profiles, before any interview is scheduled.

Tell us what you need in Qatar

Trade, number of men, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer on whether we can do it — including when the answer is no.
