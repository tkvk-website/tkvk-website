Hire steel fixers from India for the Gulf

Steel fixers from India, each given a bar bending schedule at our New Delhi centre and made to cut, bend, place and tie a cage that is then measured for spacing, laps and cover. You see how his cage measured before you interview him. We supply fixers to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the reinforcement work, not just the trade

Rebar gangs are sized and selected on a few facts most requirements leave out.

Yard or deck

Cutting and bending under a roof, or placing and tying on an open slab. Only the second loses hours in summer.

Type of structure

Towers, bridges, rafts, plant foundations or precast — say which, so experience is matched.

Schedule reading

Whether he must work from the BBS unaided. It is reported man by man.

Helper ratio

How many helpers per fixer. Skilled men carrying bar is where gang output disappears.

Pour programme

A fixed pour sequence needs a gang sized for the worst month, not the best.

Remote or city site

Far from the yard, a wrong cut cannot be replaced the same day.

What we check when a steel fixer is tested

The scheduleHe works from the bar bending schedule without being talked through it.
Cut and bendLengths and bends against the schedule, including bend radius.
Spacing, laps and coverMeasured with instruments on the finished cage — the three things an inspector checks before a pour.
Ties and rigidityTie pattern and tightness, and whether the cage keeps its shape while the pour crew climbs over it.

Certificates and papers

Steel fixing seldom comes with a formal certificate, and none is invented. The profile carries the measured assessment and an honest list of the structures each man has actually worked on. What employers ask about hiring steel fixers from India

Can they work from a bar bending schedule on their own?

That is the core of the test, and the result is given for each man rather than claimed for the whole group.

How do you check cover and spacing?

With measuring instruments on the cage he has just tied, against the schedule he was given — not by eye.

Do you supply fixers with helpers?

Yes. Tell us your gang structure and we will propose a ratio so skilled men are not cutting and carrying.

Can you supply a large gang for one pour programme?

Tell us the numbers and dates. We will say honestly how many tested men we can put forward and by when.

Have your fixers worked on high-rise?

Some have, and it is written on their profile. Where a man has only worked low-rise, the profile says that too.

Can our engineer watch the test?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video. The session is set up as real work, not an interview.

Tell us the steel fixers you need

Yard or deck, numbers, country and pour start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
