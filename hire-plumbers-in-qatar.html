Why plumbers are tested before shortlisting

Plumbing faults surface late and cost the most to fix. A joint that passes a casual look but fails a pressure test becomes a ceiling opened up after handover. The test is designed to catch that in New Delhi.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the plumbing test covers

  1. Pipework and jointing

    Threading, solvent welding, compression and press fittings as your work requires, judged on the joint rather than the speed.

  2. Pressure testing

    Whether the man tests properly and interprets the result, or signs off on a system he has not proven.

  3. Reading drawings and setting out

    Working from a layout, setting levels and falls correctly — a wrong fall is a permanent problem.

  4. Fixing and support

    Bracketing, spacing and insulation, because unsupported pipework fails later regardless of how good the joints were.

Certificates and papers

ITI Plumber certificates and previous project records go with the profile where held. Sanitary fit-out experience and mechanical or process pipework experience are stated separately.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared so the file clears the Qatar Visa Centre first time.

What employers ask about plumbers

Do you distinguish sanitary plumbers from mechanical pipework men?

Yes. They overlap but they are not the same hire, and the assessment says which one a man is.

Can they carry out pressure testing themselves?

The test includes it. A plumber who cannot test properly is not finished work, only started work.

Do you supply plumbers for maintenance contracts?

Yes, and maintenance calls for a different kind of man than new installation. Tell us which you need.

Can our MEP engineer test them by video?

Yes. We arrange the candidates, the materials and the test rig.

Tell us how many plumbers you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Qatar.
