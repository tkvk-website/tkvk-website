Why hospitality staff are tested before shortlisting

Bahrain's hospitality week is shaped by the King Fahd Causeway. A large weekend influx arrives from Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province from Thursday evening, and restaurants, hotels and leisure venues absorb it with the same headcount that served a quiet Tuesday. Anyone staffing to the weekly average is under-staffed for the half of the trade that matters.

Brigades are small. A cook here usually covers more than one station and helps with receiving and closing, and a housekeeper may also cover public areas. Breadth beats specialisation, and we are honest about which candidates actually have it.

Alcohol is served in licensed outlets, so we put that question to candidates directly and record the answer rather than leaving you to discover it in week one.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Bahrain runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Covering more than one station

    Working across the kitchen or the floor rather than sitting in a single role — the real shape of a small Bahraini outlet.

  2. The weekend surge

    Holding sequence and standard through a Thursday-to-Saturday peak several times the weekday rate.

  3. Hygiene and food safety

    Personal hygiene, temperature discipline, cross-contamination control and cleaning schedules, taught properly.

  4. Alcohol service, asked directly

    Every candidate is asked whether he will serve alcohol, and his answer is recorded in the profile before you interview him.

  5. Grooming and uniform discipline

    Set before departure, because a small property has no training department to set it afterwards.

Certificates and papers

Hotel management certificates, food handling training and prior property references go with the profile, with a plain note where experience is restaurant rather than hotel. Passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared for the LMRA work permit and visa process.

What employers ask about hospitality staff

Can you supply small numbers?

Yes. Most Bahraini requirements are small and specific, and we handle them on the same terms as large ones. A small order is harder, because one wrong hire is a whole station.

Will your staff serve alcohol?

Some will and some will not. We ask every candidate directly and record the answer in his profile, so you know before you interview rather than after he arrives.

Do you supply women for housekeeping?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your property needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the requirement and try to fill it.

Can they handle the weekend surge?

That is what we assess for here. Tell us your actual Thursday-to-Saturday covers against your weekday covers and we brief against that gap rather than an average.

Tell us how many hospitality staff you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Bahrain.
