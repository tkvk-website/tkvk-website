Why household staff are tested before they reach your home

Kuwaiti households are often large and multi-generational, with several staff and a routine that has to accommodate everybody. Fitting into that is as much of the job as the cooking or the driving.

Domestic employment in Kuwait has its own legal framework covering the contract, hours and rest day. That settles the paperwork; it says nothing about whether the man will work well in a busy household, which is what we assess.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Kuwait runs through it.

What we assess

  1. A cooking test to your household's cuisine

    The man cooks what your family eats, photographed and sent with the profile. Where the household eats several cuisines we test more than one rather than assuming.

  2. Working in a household with other staff

    Large households mean shared kitchens, overlapping duties and someone else's opinion about how it is done. We assess whether a man handles that without friction.

  3. Family driver assessment where included

    Road test, licence verified, and conduct carrying family members including children and elders. Kuwait traffic is assessed honestly.

  4. Discretion across a large family

    More people in the house means more that should not be repeated outside it. Discretion is assessed as behaviour, not promised.

What we require before we place anyone

We place men only in household roles, and we place them only where four things are in the written contract: the wage and how it is paid, the working hours and the weekly rest day, the accommodation and food, and that the worker keeps his own passport. These are not requests. A household that will not put them in writing is one we decline, because the alternative is a man stranded four thousand kilometres from home and a problem that eventually becomes yours too.

Papers and what we will not promise

Domestic employment in Kuwait runs under its own framework and the employment documents for this category need attestation by the Indian Mission. Every man also clears an approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance where his passport requires it. We confirm the current position for your case rather than assuming last year's rules still apply.

Household work carries no trade certificate, so what you receive is the assessment itself — what he cooked, how he drove, what he completed unsupervised — along with the work history we could verify and a plain note where we could not.

What households ask us

Do you supply female domestic workers?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only — cooks, houseboys, family drivers and gardeners. We tell you before taking the requirement.

Our household already has staff. Will he fit in?

That is exactly what we try to observe during preparation, and we report what we saw. We will not claim an assessment predicts how a man behaves in someone's home for two years.

Can he cook for a diwaniya?

Tell us and we test cooking for numbers separately. Feeding a family and feeding a diwaniya are different jobs and plenty of good household cooks can only do the first.

What do you need in writing from us?

Wage and how it is paid, hours and the weekly rest day, accommodation and food, and that the man keeps his own passport. Those four, in writing, before we proceed.
