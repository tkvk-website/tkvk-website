Why testing in India matters for Bahrain

Bahrain is a smaller market where employers are usually hiring in small, specific numbers. That makes accuracy matter more than volume: one unsuitable man in a team of five is a serious problem.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into Bahrain works

  1. LMRA work visa against a named employer

    Work visas are issued through the Labour Market Regulatory Authority and tied to your establishment. We supply against the categories you are permitted to recruit for.

  2. Medical and document clearance

    Medical fitness from an approved centre in India, with trade and education documents prepared in the form Bahraini processing expects.

  3. Terms explained before signing

    Salary, hours, accommodation and food are explained to the worker in his own language during the pre-departure briefing.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    Bahrain is ECR-notified. ECR passport holders need Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure, handled by Taj HR Services as the licensed agent.

What Bahraini employers ask us

We only need four or five men. Is that too small?

No. Small technical requirements are where testing matters most, because there is no room to carry a weak man. We handle small orders on the same terms as large ones.

Can we test candidates before committing?

Yes — in person at our New Delhi centre or by video. We arrange the candidates and the workshop.

Do you supply only construction trades?

No. Facility management, hospitality and retail staff are a large part of what we prepare, and they are tested for the work they will actually do.

What are your commercial terms?

Ask and we will send them in writing — what is charged, what is included, and what happens if a worker does not perform.

Tell us what you need in Bahrain

Trade, number of men, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer on whether we can do it — including when the answer is no.
