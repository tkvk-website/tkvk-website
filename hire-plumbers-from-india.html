Hire plumbers from India for the Gulf

Plumbers from India, each made to make a joint, set falls from a layout and hold a pressure test at our New Delhi centre before you see his profile. Sanitary fit-out and process pipework are reported separately, because they are not the same trade. We supply plumbers to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

A building plumber put on process lines is a problem you find at commissioning. These details decide who is shortlisted.

Sanitary or process

Bathrooms and building services, or mechanical and process pipework.

Jointing method

Threaded, solvent-weld, compression or press-fit — the ones your job uses.

Finish standard

A photograph of an accepted installation, if your client inspects what is visible.

Helpers

The gang structure you plan, so skilled men are not sent to carry pipe.

Plumbers for each Gulf country

Most plumbing is indoors, so the midday summer stop matters mainly for roof, external and excavation work. The bigger differences are scale, finish, water and climate.

Plumbers for Saudi ArabiaTypical work: Building MEP, sanitary fit-out and bathroom pods, villas and compounds, hotels, plant pipework.
No open-air work: 12:00–15:00, 15 June–15 September.
Giga-project packages repeat hundreds of identical bathrooms, so consistency is reported separately.
Plumbers for the UAETypical work: High-rise plumbing, fit-out, facility management, villas, hotels and retail.
No open-air work: 12:30–15:00, 15 June–15 September.
Clients inspect the visible finish; careless chrome-work comes back on the snag list. Tower experience is stated per man.
Plumbers for OmanTypical work: Building and fit-out, villas and compounds, facility services, refurbishment, remote camp installations.
No open-air work: 12:30–15:30, 1 June–31 August.
Hard water and scaling are routine, and accurate take-off is weighted heavily because materials are far away.
Plumbers for QatarTypical work: Building and fit-out, MEP and sanitary works, infrastructure, hotels, facility services.
No open-air work: 10:00–15:30, 1 June–15 September.
First-fix and fit-out move indoors for the long summer stop; say whether you need internal or external men.
Plumbers for KuwaitTypical work: Maintenance and alteration, building and fit-out, facility services, housing, commercial buildings.
No open-air work: 11:00–16:00, 1 June–31 August.
Old pipework often differs from the drawing; fault-tracing and reporting what he finds are part of the test.
Plumbers for BahrainTypical work: Building and fit-out, villas, facility services, refurbishment, hotels and retail.
No open-air work: 12:00–16:00, mid-June–end of August.
Humid, salty air and hard water: fixing choice and treating a seized fitting as a symptom are assessed. Small numbers are normal.

What employers ask about hiring plumbers from India

Do you supply sanitary plumbers and process pipe men separately?

Yes. The assessment states which each man is, because the two are not interchangeable.

Can they work to our pressure-testing procedure?

Send it and we test to it. If a man has only ever done a visual check, the assessment says so.

Our finish standard is strict. Can you test to it?

Send the standard or a photo of an accepted installation; we test against it and tell you who met it.

Do you supply plumbers with helpers?

Yes. Tell us your gang structure and we will propose a ratio.

Can our MEP engineer test them before selection?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video, with a test rig rather than a question-and-answer session.

We only need two or three plumbers. Will you take that?

Yes. Small numbers are normal and are handled on the same terms as large ones.

Tell us the plumbers you need

Sanitary or process, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
