Why AC and HVAC technicians are tested before shortlisting

In the UAE air conditioning is a year-round service load, not a seasonal one, and the market is built around service-level agreements. A callback that should have been closed on the first visit is not just an annoyance, it eats the margin on the contract and shows up in your KPI report.

District and central chilled-water cooling carries a very large share of the load in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, which changes what a technician needs to know. Plant rooms, heat exchangers, pumps, valves and BTU metering are a different discipline from unitary service, and the man who is excellent on a rooftop package may be lost in a basement plant room.

So we test diagnosis, and we record separately whether a man has real central-plant exposure or only unitary. Those two answers decide whether he is useful to you, and no certificate distinguishes them.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the trade test covers

  1. Fault-finding on a live unit

    A unit with a fault, and the man has to find it. The route he takes to the answer tells you more than the answer does.

  2. Gas handling and pressure work

    Charging, recovery, leak detection and gauge reading, and whether he handles refrigerant cleanly.

  3. Brazing and pipework

    Copper joint quality, insulation and support — the work that decides whether the system leaks in year two.

  4. Electrical and controls

    Contactors, capacitors, thermostats and control wiring, tested rather than asked about.

  5. Central plant versus unitary

    Whether he has genuinely worked chilled water, pumps, heat exchangers and plant rooms, or only split and package units.

Refrigerants: what we actually check

One thing worth asking every candidate, and worth us testing: which refrigerants he has actually worked on. A man whose whole career was R-22 is not automatically safe on R-410A or R-32. The operating pressures are higher, the oil is different — POE rather than mineral — so contamination and moisture matter far more, and R-32 is an A2L, mildly flammable, which changes how he brazes, recovers and stores it. Gulf fleets are mixed: old R-22 units still running alongside newer R-410A and R-32 equipment, often in the same building. We record which refrigerants each man has genuine hands-on time with, and we say plainly where he has none, because "AC technician" on a CV tells you nothing about this.

What employers ask about AC and HVAC technicians

Can they work in a district cooling plant room?

Ask for it specifically. Plant-room work is a different discipline from unitary service and only some of our men have real exposure to it. We name who does and who does not.

How do you judge whether he can actually diagnose?

We hand him a unit with a fault and watch. Speed matters less than method — the man who checks systematically is the one who closes a callback on the first visit.

Do you supply women technicians?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can you supply a technician with a helper?

Yes, and on maintenance rounds it is often the cheaper mix. Tell us the workload and we will propose the pairing.

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Oman.
