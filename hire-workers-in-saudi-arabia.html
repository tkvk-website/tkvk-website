Why testing in India matters for Saudi Arabia

Saudi projects run on programme dates, and a man who cannot hold the standard on day one puts those dates at risk. We test in India against the standard you name, so the men you interview have already done the work once.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into Saudi Arabia works

  1. Musaned is where the file lives

    Recruitment of Indian workers is routed through the Musaned platform. The contract is registered there and the worker sees the same terms you entered, which removes most on-arrival disputes about salary and hours.

  2. Attestation of your demand documents

    Your demand letter, power of attorney and job order are attested through the Saudi chamber and the Indian mission before the visa work can proceed. Getting this right first time saves weeks.

  3. Medical from an approved centre

    Workers must be cleared at a GCC-approved medical centre in India. We sequence the medical so it does not expire while the visa is being stamped.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    Saudi Arabia is an ECR-notified country, so ECR passport holders need Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure. This is handled by Taj HR Services as the licensed agent.

What Saudi employers ask us

Do you supply against Nitaqat and Saudization limits?

Your quota and Saudization band decide how many visas you hold and in which categories. We work inside what you already have — tell us the visa categories issued to you and we supply against those, rather than sending profiles that cannot be visaed.

Can our engineer test the men in India?

Yes. Send your technical representative to our centre in New Delhi, or run the test over video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the consumables. Many Saudi contractors prefer this, and we prefer it too.

Are certificates enough, or is a practical test necessary?

A certificate tells you what a man studied. It does not tell you whether his weld passes visual inspection or whether he works safely at height. That is why every candidate is practically tested before he reaches your shortlist.

How do you handle replacement if a worker does not perform?

Ask us for our replacement terms in writing before you commit. We would rather state them plainly than let you assume something we have not agreed.
