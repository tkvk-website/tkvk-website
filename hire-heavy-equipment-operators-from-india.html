Licences and papers

Indian driving and operator licences are verified for category and validity and sent with the profile, with a clear split between machines a man is licensed for and machines he has only operated. An Indian licence is not a Gulf licence in any of the six countries; the local licence or test is arranged on your side after arrival. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. What employers ask about hiring heavy equipment operators from India

Will his Indian licence be valid in the Gulf?

Not by itself in any of the six countries. The local licence follows local testing and is arranged on your side; we prepare and verify his Indian papers.

Which machines do you test for?

Tell us the machine. Each man is tested on what he claims, and machines he has only operated without a licence are named as such.

Do you supply certified crane operators?

Ask with the crane type and capacity. We say honestly what we have and do not stretch a licence to fit.

Can you mobilise operators in bulk?

Tell us numbers, machines and start date. The schedule is built on your visa quota and attestation position, and we say plainly when a date will not hold.

How do we keep operators beyond six months?

Tell them the truth about the camp, rotation and wage before they sign, and put the wage in writing. We brief on all three.

Can you send a mechanic with the operators?

Yes. On a larger fleet it is worth doing; tell us the fleet and we will propose a mix.

Tell us the heavy equipment operators you need

Machine, site, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
