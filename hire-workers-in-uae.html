Why testing in India matters for the UAE

In the UAE the worker signs your job offer before he flies, and the contract must match it. That makes clarity at the start worth more than speed. We make sure the man understands the terms in his own language and can actually do the job he signed for.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into the UAE works

  1. MoHRE job offer, signed in India

    The employer files the job offer with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, and the worker signs it before departure. The employment contract issued later has to match that offer.

  2. Entry permit, then Emirates ID and medical

    The work entry permit is issued against your establishment. Medical fitness and Emirates ID biometrics are completed after arrival, but the Indian-side medical still has to be cleared first.

  3. Wage Protection System

    Salaries are paid through WPS. Because the wage is visible and fixed from the offer stage, the figure you quote us is the figure the worker will see — so quote the real one.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    The UAE is ECR-notified. Workers on ECR passports need Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure, handled by Taj HR Services as the licensed agent.

What UAE employers ask us

Can we interview candidates by video?

Yes, and most UAE employers do. You get test evidence and profiles first, then interview from your office. Our centre arranges the candidates and the connection at your timezone.

Do workers arrive knowing what they signed?

That is the point of the pre-departure briefing. Salary, hours, overtime, accommodation and food are explained in the worker’s own language before he signs, so the MoHRE contract holds no surprises.

Can you supply small numbers, not just bulk?

Yes. A single wrong technician on a fit-out job is a bigger problem than ten wrong labourers. Tell us the number and we will say honestly whether we can serve it well.

How much working English will the men have?

Enough to take an instruction, read a safety sign and report a problem. We do not claim fluency, and we will tell you where a particular man is weak.

Tell us what you need in the UAE

Trade, number of men, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer on whether we can do it — including when the answer is no.
