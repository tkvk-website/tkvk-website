ECR and ECNR passports: what they mean when you hire from India

Every Indian passport falls into one of two categories. ECR holders need government emigration clearance before they can take a job in the Gulf; ECNR holders do not. Some skilled tradesmen are ECR, so the category affects your paperwork and your timeline. Here is what it means, in plain terms.

Who is ECNR Send a requirement

ECR and ECNR in one paragraph

Under India’s Emigration Act, 1983, a citizen going abroad for work in certain countries needs emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants, an office of the Ministry of External Affairs. This applies only to people whose passport is endorsed ECR — Emigration Check Required. Everyone else is ECNR — Emigration Check Not Required. Since 2007, passports carry an endorsement only for ECR holders, so a passport with no ECR notation is ECNR.

The Ministry lists 19 ECR countries. All six Gulf states are among them: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Who is ECNR

A person is ECNR, and needs no emigration clearance, if he falls into any of these groups:

  • Passed the 10th standard (matriculation) or any higher qualification
  • Holds a two-year NCVT or SCVT diploma, or a three-year polytechnic diploma
  • Holds a professional degree, such as engineering or medicine
  • Pays income tax, or is a gazetted government servant
  • Is over 50 years of age
  • Has lived abroad for more than three years in total, and his spouse
  • Holds a permanent immigration visa for a country such as the UK, USA or Australia
  • Seamen with a CDC, nurses with a recognised qualification, and some other listed groups

What emigration clearance involves

It runs on eMigrate

Clearance is granted online through the eMigrate system. The foreign employer has to be registered there first, and the demand, job details and employment contract are linked to each worker. See eMigrate registration for foreign employers.

Through a licensed recruiting agent, or directly

Most employers recruit through an Indian recruiting agent registered with the Ministry of External Affairs; the Ministry warns workers not to deal with unregistered sub-agents. Employers can also recruit directly once registered on eMigrate.

Insurance is compulsory

Every ECR worker must hold the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana policy. It costs Rs 275 for two years or Rs 375 for three, and covers up to Rs 10 lakh for accidental death or permanent disability, medical treatment up to Rs 1 lakh, and repatriation if the worker is found medically unfit or the job ends early.

Passport validity

The Ministry advises workers to carry a passport valid for at least six months, together with the employment contract signed by both sides.

What it means for your hiring

Do not filter out ECR candidates. Some of the best site hands are ECR simply because they left school early. The clearance is a routine government step, but it is an extra step, so allow for it in the start date. ECNR candidates — for example ITI-trained tradesmen with a two-year NCVT certificate — skip it.

Common questions

What does ECR mean on an Indian passport?

ECR stands for Emigration Check Required. A worker whose passport carries the ECR endorsement needs emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants before he can travel on an employment visa to an ECR country, which includes all six Gulf states.

How do I know if a passport is ECR or ECNR?

Passports issued from 2007 onwards show an endorsement only when the holder is ECR. A passport with no ECR notation is ECNR.

Who is ECNR?

Among others: anyone who has passed the 10th standard (matriculation) or higher, holders of a two-year NCVT or SCVT diploma or a three-year polytechnic diploma, professional degree holders, income-tax payers, people over 50, and people who have lived abroad for more than three years in total.

Which countries need emigration clearance?

The Ministry of External Affairs lists 19 ECR countries, including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. Clearance is needed when an ECR passport holder goes to one of them for employment.

Is insurance compulsory for ECR workers?

Yes. The Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana is mandatory for ECR workers. It costs Rs 275 for two years or Rs 375 for three years and covers up to Rs 10 lakh for accidental death or permanent disability, plus medical and repatriation benefits.
