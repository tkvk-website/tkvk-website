Why testing in India matters for Oman

Oman clears workers against the employer’s approved requirement, so documents have to be ready before the clearance stage rather than after it. Preparation in India is what keeps the timeline honest.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into Oman works

  1. Labour clearance against your requirement

    The Ministry of Labour issues clearance for the categories you are approved for. We supply against those categories, not around them.

  2. Employment visa and arrival formalities

    The employment visa is issued on the strength of that clearance. Residence and labour card formalities follow on arrival, and the Indian-side paperwork has to support them.

  3. Medical and police clearance

    Medical fitness from an approved centre in India, plus police clearance where required. Both are timed so neither lapses before the visa is used.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    Oman is ECR-notified, so ECR passport holders require Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure. Taj HR Services handles this as the licensed agent.

What Omani employers ask us

How early should we start the paperwork?

As soon as your clearance categories are confirmed. Attestation and medical validity are the two things that most often force a repeat, and both are avoidable with sequencing.

Do you supply for Omanisation-restricted categories?

We supply only into categories you are permitted to recruit for. Tell us your approved list and we will work inside it.

What evidence of skill will we receive?

The assessment sheet from the practical test, photographs or video of the test piece where the trade allows it, and the man’s trade and experience papers.

Can we test the candidates ourselves?

Yes, in person at our New Delhi centre or by video. We arrange candidates, workshop and material.

Tell us what you need in Oman

Trade, number of men, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer on whether we can do it — including when the answer is no.
