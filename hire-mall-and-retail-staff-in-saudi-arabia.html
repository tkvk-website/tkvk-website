Why retail support staff are assessed before you interview them

Saudi retail has changed shape. Since 19 January 2026 the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development requires a 60 percent Saudi quota in sales occupations, and Retail Sales Representative is named on that list, applying to any establishment with three or more sales employees. That is a hiring rule, not a detail, and it decides which of your vacancies an Indian worker can lawfully fill.

So we do not offer you shop-floor sales staff. We prepare and supply the roles behind the sale: stockroom and replenishment, goods receiving at the loading bay, trolley and car-park attendants, food-court clearing and dish sections, and mall porters. Tell us the job title on your visa block and we will tell you plainly whether it sits inside or outside the nationalised list before anyone is shortlisted.

The rest is operational. Saudi malls trade late and trade hardest in Ramadan, when replenishment moves to the small hours and the back corridor becomes the busiest part of the building. A stockroom man who has only worked day shifts is a liability in that month.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Saudi Arabia runs through it.

What we assess

  1. Replenishment and stock accuracy

    Reading a planogram, facing and rotating stock, and finding the item a floor colleague is asking for without emptying three cages onto the corridor.

  2. Goods receiving and the loading bay

    Checking a delivery against the note, spotting damage before it is signed for, and moving cages and pallets through a service corridor without blocking it.

  3. Handheld scanner and stock system

    Scanning in, scanning out, and flagging a mismatch rather than letting it pass. Tell us your system and we brief on the workflow, not the brand.

  4. Night and Ramadan shift discipline

    Working the replenishment window after closing, and holding the same standard at four in the morning as at four in the afternoon.

  5. Conduct in front of customers

    What to do when a shopper stops him in the aisle, and when to hand the question to a Saudi colleague on the sales side rather than answer it himself.

What we require before we place anyone

Mall work is public work. A man on a shop floor or in a food court is seen by your customers all day, so we assess conduct the way we assess the task: how he handles being watched, being interrupted, being told he is wrong by a shopper, and being asked something he does not know the answer to. We place men only, and we place them only where the wage, the working hours and the weekly rest day, the accommodation and the shift pattern are set out in writing before he signs. Retail runs on split shifts and late closings more than almost any other sector, and a man who was not told that before he left India is a man who leaves within a year.

Papers and what we will not promise

Retail support work carries no trade certificate and we do not invent one. You receive an honest statement of prior retail or warehouse experience, the preparation completed, and full medical and document status. Passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared so the file clears the Saudi visa process first time.

What retailers ask us

Can you supply shop-floor sales staff?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who says they can. Sales occupations carry a 60 percent Saudi quota that took effect on 19 January 2026, with Retail Sales Representative named on the list. We supply the support roles around the sale, and we tell you which of your titles are affected before you commit to a visa block.

Do you supply women for retail?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we tell you that at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can they work night replenishment through Ramadan?

That is exactly what we brief for. Tell us the shift pattern and we assess and prepare for it, and we make sure the man is told the pattern in India rather than discovering it in Riyadh.

How many can you mobilise for a new store opening?

Tell us the number and the opening date. We give you a realistic schedule based on your visa quota and attestation position, not a number designed to win the order.

Tell us what your stores need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Saudi Arabia.
