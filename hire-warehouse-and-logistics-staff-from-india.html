Hire warehouse staff from India for the Gulf

Pickers, storekeepers, loaders and material controllers from India, put through a real pick list and stock count at our New Delhi centre — we count the errors rather than describe a man as careful. Storekeepers are graded separately from loaders, because that difference is most of the value. We supply warehouse staff to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the store and the role

A man who handled a small godown is not automatically ready for a scanner and a pick-rate target. These details decide the shortlist.

Role

Picker, loader, storekeeper or material controller.

Type of stock

Retail cartons, food and cold chain, construction material, spares or pharmacy.

Systems

Handheld scanners and the WMS your site uses.

Mixed duties

In small stores one man may pick, count, load, do the paperwork and sometimes drive.

Shift pattern

Night dispatch or long shifts in a hot yard, told to men before they sign.

Forklifts

If machine work is needed, operators are tested separately.

Pick accuracyRight item, quantity and location from a real pick list, with errors counted under light time pressure.

What employers ask about hiring warehouse staff from India

Do they have forklift experience?

Some do, named with the machine. If you need operators, they are tested separately rather than folded into a general batch.

Can they use a scanner and a WMS?

Tell us the system and we run a scan-and-pick flow on a device. Most learn quickly; the profile says who did not.

We need storekeepers, not just loaders. Can you tell the difference?

Yes. Numeracy, documents and the temperament to say the count is wrong are graded separately.

Can one man handle store work and driving?

Where the job needs it, both are assessed, and a licence is verified rather than assumed.

How many can you supply for a new distribution centre?

Tell us the number and date. We say honestly what we can prepare and test properly in that window.

Can you supply small numbers?

Yes. Three or four men is a normal requirement.

Tell us the warehouse staff you need

Roles, type of stock, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
