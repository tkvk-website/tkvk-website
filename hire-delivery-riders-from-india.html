What employers ask about hiring delivery riders from India

Can an expatriate rider work for us directly in Saudi Arabia?

Only inside a licensed light transport company under the TGA decision. If you are not that company, you contract one, and the rider’s contract follows that.

Is his Indian licence valid in the Gulf?

No. Conversion or a local test comes after arrival, and that period belongs in your plan.

How do you judge safe riding rather than fast riding?

By watching him ride under a time pressure he knows about. The assessment says whether he keeps his distance when behind schedule.

Can they use a delivery app?

It is tested and graded separately, because phone skills vary more than riding skill in this pool.

Can they work night or split shifts?

Men are told the real rota at interview; those who accept nights knowing they are nights are the ones who stay.

Can you supply riders in bulk for a launch?

Tell us numbers and date. We build the schedule on your visa quota and say plainly if the date will not hold.
