What to send so the first reply is useful

A requirement that arrives complete gets a real answer the same time it is read. One that arrives as “we need welders” takes three messages to get to the same place.

  1. Trade and standard

    The trade, and the codes, positions or standards your project works to. A 6G pipe welder and a plate welder are different men.

  2. Numbers and date

    How many, and when you need them on site. Tell us the real date rather than a comfortable one, so the schedule we give back is honest.

  3. Salary and terms

    Salary, hours, overtime, accommodation and food. The worker sees these before he signs, so quote what you will actually pay.

  4. Country and visa position

    Which Gulf country, and the visa categories or quota you already hold. We supply inside what you have rather than sending profiles that cannot be visaed.

Visiting the centre

Employers are welcome to come to the New Delhi centre and watch a trade test run, or send a technical representative to conduct their own. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the consumables. Tell us roughly when you want to come and what you want tested, and we will have it ready rather than improvised on the day.

If travelling does not suit, the same test runs over video and you see the test piece and assessment either way.
