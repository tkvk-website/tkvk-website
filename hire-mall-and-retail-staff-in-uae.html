Why retail support staff are assessed before you interview them

A UAE mall is not a shop with a car park. Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates run as small cities, with trading hours that stretch past midnight on weekends, a service corridor that never stops, and access control that treats a stockroom man as a badged contractor rather than a visitor. The job is as much about moving through that building correctly as about the task itself.

The calendar is the other half of it. Dubai Shopping Festival, Ramadan and Eid, and the summer months when the entire population moves indoors, each produce a different kind of pressure — volume, night trading, and sheer footfall density. A man who has only worked a steady Indian retail floor has not met any of them.

We prepare for the building and the calendar together: mall induction and badge discipline, working a corridor shared with a dozen other tenants, and holding a standard in front of a crowd. The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services and every deployment runs through it.

What we assess

  1. Mall induction and access discipline

    Badges, service lifts, delivery windows and the corridors a tenant's staff may and may not use. Most mall complaints against retail staff are access breaches, not work failures.

  2. Replenishment under footfall

    Filling shelves while the aisle is full of shoppers — cage placement, aisle clearance and knowing when to stop and wait rather than push through.

  3. Food court and dining clearance

    Table turnaround, tray and waste separation, spill response and the speed a crowded court needs at one in the afternoon.

  4. Long and split shifts

    Ten- and twelve-hour trading days, split rosters and weekend late closings — assessed for stamina and for the honesty to say when he is not fit to continue.

  5. English on the floor

    Enough working English to take an instruction, answer a simple shopper question and direct someone to the right counter. We grade it honestly rather than claim fluency.

What we require before we place anyone

Mall work is public work. A man on a shop floor or in a food court is seen by your customers all day, so we assess conduct the way we assess the task: how he handles being watched, being interrupted, being told he is wrong by a shopper, and being asked something he does not know the answer to. We place men only, and we place them only where the wage, the working hours and the weekly rest day, the accommodation and the shift pattern are set out in writing before he signs. Retail runs on split shifts and late closings more than almost any other sector, and a man who was not told that before he left India is a man who leaves within a year.

Papers and what we will not promise

There is no certificate for this category and we do not manufacture one. What you get is the assessment itself, an honest grade of working English, verified prior experience where it exists and a plain note where it does not, plus passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance prepared for the UAE process.

What retailers ask us

Do your men have mall experience or only shop experience?

Both exist and they are not the same thing. We tell you which one a given man has. Someone who has worked an Indian standalone store has never handled a service corridor shared with sixty tenants, and we will not describe him as if he has.

Do you supply women for retail?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can they handle Dubai Shopping Festival and Ramadan hours?

Tell us the roster you actually run in those periods and we assess and brief against it. The critical part is that the man is told the hours in India, in writing, before he signs.

Can you supply a supervisor with the team?

Ask. A replenishment team without someone who can hold the standard at eleven at night drifts within a month, whatever the quality of the individuals.

Tell us what your stores need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to the UAE.
