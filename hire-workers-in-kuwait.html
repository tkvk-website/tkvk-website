Why testing in India matters for Kuwait

In Kuwait the two things that delay a mobilisation are quota and attestation. Neither can be rushed at the end, and both can be managed at the start. That is where our work goes.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment runs through it. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

How hiring from India into Kuwait works

  1. Work permit against your quota

    Permits are issued against the manpower authorisation you hold. We supply against the categories in that authorisation so no profile is wasted.

  2. Attestation of certificates

    Educational and trade certificates generally require attestation before the permit and visa stage. This takes real time, so we open it as soon as a candidate is confirmed rather than at the end.

  3. Medical from an approved centre

    Workers are cleared at a GCC-approved medical centre in India, timed so validity still holds when the visa is used.

  4. PoE clearance on eMigrate

    Kuwait is ECR-notified. ECR passport holders require Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure, handled by Taj HR Services as the licensed agent.

What Kuwaiti employers ask us

How long does attestation take?

It varies with the issuing state and the document. We will tell you the realistic range for the specific candidates once we see their papers, rather than quoting an average that may not apply.

Can you supply against a specific job title on our authorisation?

Yes, and we prefer it. Give us the exact title and category from your authorisation and we will test and supply to match it.

Do you charge the workers?

Indian law caps what a worker may be charged, and many employers now follow employer-pays recruitment. Tell us your policy and we will put the cost structure in writing before starting.

What if a candidate fails our interview?

You see more candidates. Nobody is pushed at you as a package, and we will tell you if we think the requirement itself needs adjusting.

Tell us what you need in Kuwait

Trade, number of men, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer on whether we can do it — including when the answer is no.
