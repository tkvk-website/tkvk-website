Why packing helpers are assessed before shortlisting

Packing looks like the job anyone can do, which is exactly why it is hired carelessly and then costs money. A slow packer holds up a line; an inaccurate one sends the wrong carton; one who mislabels a batch can cost you a recall.

Saudi lines run at volume and they run long, with seasonal peaks that do not wait. What matters is not whether a man can pack, but whether he is still packing at the same rate and the same accuracy in the sixth hour.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Saudi Arabia runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Pack rate held over time

    We measure units packed in a timed run, then measure again later in the session. The second number is the useful one — plenty of men start fast and drop by a third, and we write down which ones.

  2. Label, batch and expiry accuracy

    Matching the right label to the right product, and reading batch and expiry codes correctly. This is the single error in packing that turns into a recall rather than a complaint, so we test it separately and count the mistakes.

  3. Food hygiene discipline

    Hand washing, gloves, hairnet, no jewellery, and what he does when he drops something. We test the habit rather than asking whether he knows the rule, because everyone knows the rule.

  4. Carton counts and seal quality

    Counting into a carton without recounting three times, and sealing so the pack survives a pallet and a truck. Weak seals become damaged stock at the far end and nobody can prove where it happened.

Papers and what we will not promise

There is no trade certificate for packing work, and we will not invent one. What you get is the assessment record — pack rate, error count, hygiene observations — plus any genuine food-handling certificate a man actually holds, named as such, and a plain note where he holds none.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about packing helpers

Can they work on a fast automated line?

Tell us the line speed and we assess against it rather than in the abstract. A man comfortable at hand-packing pace is not automatically comfortable feeding a machine that does not slow down for him, and we say which one we are sending you.

Do they understand food safety rules?

We cover hygiene in preparation and test what stuck. Where a man holds a real food-handling certificate we name it; where he does not, the profile says so instead of implying training he never had.

We need them for a seasonal peak. Can you supply quickly?

Sourcing and preparation run in parallel with your document attestation. Send the requirement with the date you need them on the line and we come back with a dated plan against it, not a general promise.

Can packers also do warehouse and loading work?

Often yes, and it is a common combined role. Say what the second duty is and we assess that too, rather than sending a packer who is surprised to be loading a truck.
