What a Gulf employer has to send

Recruiting from India runs on four documents from your side. Get them right the first time and the visa stage moves; get one wrong and you lose weeks to re-attestation. Here is what each one is and what it has to say.

Send your requirement Common questions

The four documents

These come from you, on your letterhead, and they are what allow a licensed Indian agent to recruit on your behalf. We check the set before it goes for attestation, because a rejection at that stage costs you the calendar, not just the fee.

  1. Demand letter

    Your formal statement of what you need: the trades, the number of workers in each, salary, working hours, overtime terms, contract length, accommodation, food, medical cover and air passage. It is the document the worker’s terms are ultimately traced back to, so vague wording here creates disputes later.

  2. Power of attorney

    Authorises a named Indian recruiting agent to act for you — to advertise, interview, select and process the workers in your demand letter. It names one agent, for one requirement, and it is what the Protector of Emigrants looks for when the file reaches them.

  3. Job order or employment agreement

    The terms as the worker will sign them. In several Gulf countries the worker signs this before he flies and the contract issued on arrival has to match it, so the figures in it should be the figures you will actually pay.

  4. Guarantee or undertaking letter

    Your confirmation that the stated terms will be honoured — salary paid, accommodation provided, and the worker not passed to a third party. Some countries fold this into the demand letter; others want it separately.

Attestation: where most time is lost

Your documents generally need attesting by your local chamber of commerce and then by the Indian mission in your country before the Indian side can act on them. That sequence takes real time, and it cannot be compressed at the end.

Check before you attest

A wrong job title, a salary that contradicts the job order, or a missing signature means starting the attestation again. Send us the drafts first — we read them against what your country actually requires and tell you what to fix while fixing is still free.

Requirements differ by country

What Saudi Arabia wants attested is not identical to what Kuwait or Oman wants, and the detail changes from time to time. We confirm the current position for your country rather than working from a template.

Start it early

Attestation runs in parallel with sourcing and testing, not after it. Opening it as soon as the requirement is confirmed is usually the difference between meeting your date and missing it.

What happens on our side

Once your documents are in order, the Indian side of the process runs through Taj HR Services, holder of Government of India recruitment licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. You can verify that number yourself on the eMigrate portal before dealing with anyone.

Worker-side documents

Passport and ECR status, medical fitness from an approved centre, police clearance where required, and attestation of trade or education certificates. We sequence these so none expires while your visa is being processed.

PoE clearance

All six Gulf countries are ECR-notified, so workers on ECR passports need Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate before departure. This is handled by the licensed agent. Anyone offering to skip it is putting your worker and your visa at risk.

What employers ask about the paperwork

Does the demand letter have to be attested?

For most Gulf countries yes, through your chamber of commerce and the Indian mission in your country. Requirements differ by country and change from time to time, so we confirm what yours needs before you spend on attestation.

Can we issue a power of attorney to more than one agent?

You can, but it splits accountability and usually slows everything down. One named agent per requirement is cleaner, and it is what the emigration system expects.

What happens if our documents are rejected?

You lose the attestation time, not the requirement. We check the set before it goes for attestation precisely so that does not happen, and we tell you exactly what was wrong if it does.

Do we need to register on eMigrate ourselves?

Foreign employers register on the eMigrate portal to be recognised in the system. We will tell you what your side must do and what runs through the licensed agent on ours.

How long does the paperwork stage take?

It depends on your country, your chamber, and the Indian mission there — not on anything an agency controls. We give you a realistic range for your case at the start and tell you the moment something slips.
