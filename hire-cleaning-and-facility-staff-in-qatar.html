Why cleaning and facility staff are tested before shortlisting

Facility contracts are won on a bid and kept on consistency. The difference between a renewed contract and a lost one is whether the standard holds on the hundredth day, which comes from method training rather than supervision alone.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Method and sequence

    Cleaning in the right order with the right equipment, so the result holds up to an inspection rather than looking clean for an hour.

  2. Chemical handling and safety

    Dilution, labelling, what must never be mixed, and PPE. Chemical incidents on facility contracts are almost always training failures.

  3. Machine use

    Scrubbers, buffers, vacuums and pressure washers where the contract needs them, taught on the machine and not on paper.

  4. Conduct in occupied buildings

    Working around residents, patients, visitors and staff, with discretion, uniform discipline and reporting rather than concealing a problem.

Certificates and papers

This category rarely carries formal certification and we do not invent any. You receive an honest statement of prior experience, the preparation completed, and full medical and document status.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared so the file clears the Qatar Visa Centre first time.

What employers ask about cleaning and facility staff

Do you supply in bulk for facility contracts?

Yes, and this is the category where bulk mobilisation is most common. Tell us the number and the start date and we will give you a realistic schedule.

Are workers briefed on conduct in Qatar?

Yes. Workplace conduct, local norms and site rules are part of the pre-departure briefing, along with their contract terms.

Can you supply supervisors for these teams?

Ask. A team without a capable supervisor underperforms regardless of how good the individual workers are.

Can you supply female housekeeping staff?

Tell us the requirement and the accommodation and supervision arrangements, and we will answer honestly whether we can serve it properly.

Tell us how many cleaning and facility staff you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Qatar.
