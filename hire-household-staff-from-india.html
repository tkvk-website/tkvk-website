Hire male household staff from India for the Gulf

Male cooks, houseboys, family drivers and gardeners from India for Gulf households. A cook cooks your family’s cuisine at our New Delhi centre and the food is photographed; a driver is road-tested. Men are placed only where the contract is written down. We supply household staff to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us about the household

Household staff live with the family, so fit matters as much as skill. These details decide the shortlist.

Roles

Cook, houseboy, family driver or gardener — or one man covering several.

Cuisine

Arabic, Indian, continental or a mix, and whether he must cook for guests in numbers.

Other staff

Whether he will work alongside other household staff.

Children and elders

For drivers especially: school runs, child seats and passenger care.

Location

A city villa or an isolated farm or estate, told to him before he agrees.

Garden

Lawns, date palms or irrigation — not the same work.

Papers and the written contract

Household work has no trade certificate, so the profile carries what he cooked, how he drove and what he completed unsupervised, with verified work history. We place men only where four things are in the written contract: the wage and how it is paid, working hours and the weekly rest day, accommodation and food, and that the worker keeps his own passport. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. Recruitment and deployment are carried out by Taj HR Services under Government of India licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025.

What employers ask about hiring male household staff from India

Do you supply housemaids or nannies?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only — cooks, houseboys, family drivers and gardeners — and says so at the start.

Can one man cook and drive?

Often, and it is a common combined role. Both are tested properly: a cooking test and a road test.

Can he cook Arabic and Indian food?

Some can and many cannot. Tell us both matter and both are tested, with photographs.

What do you require from the household?

A written contract covering wage and payment, hours and weekly rest day, accommodation and food, and that he keeps his own passport.

What if he does not suit the household?

Ask for our replacement terms and you get them in writing before you commit.

Can he cook for a large gathering?

Tell us and cooking for numbers is tested separately from family meals.

Tell us the male household staff you need

Roles, cuisine, household size, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
