Say which electrician you mean

The same job title covers a villa wireman and a plant electrician. These details decide which of the two reaches your shortlist.

Building or industrial

Housing and fit-out wiring, or motors, control panels and industrial power. The assessment names which one each man is.

Low or high voltage

Say if HV exposure is required. It is a narrow pool and we will not stretch the description to fit.

Drawings

Whether he must work from English schematics without being walked through them.

New build or live building

Alteration inside an occupied building rewards fault-finding over speed.

Inspection standard

The authority or client standard your work is inspected against, so we test to it.

Site conditions

Towers, remote sites on generator supply, or coastal plant where corrosion is routine.

What we check when an electrician is tested

Wiring from a schematicHe is handed a simple drawing and wires it. Reading it unaided tells you most of what matters.
Panel terminationCrimping, lugging, glanding and cable dressing — untidy terminations are what Gulf supervisors notice first.
MeasurementContinuity, insulation resistance with a megger and correct multimeter use. Guessing is marked down.
Safe isolationLock-out tag-out, proving dead before touching, and refusing an unsafe instruction.

What employers ask about hiring electricians from India

Do you separate domestic, commercial and industrial electricians?

Yes. A man who has wired villas is not ready for a plant room, so we test to the level your work needs and say where each candidate actually sits.

Can they work from drawings in English?

Reading a schematic is part of the test, and the result is reported for each man rather than claimed for all of them.

Do you supply electricians with high-voltage experience?

Ask with your requirement and you will hear what we genuinely have. HV experience is never overstated.

Can our engineer interview and test them by video?

Yes. We arrange the candidates, the panel and the instruments, and your engineer runs the session from the Gulf.

Will they meet our local inspection standard?

Send the standard your work is inspected against and we will test to it. We do not claim familiarity with an authority’s rules that a man has never worked under.

We only need two or three electricians. Is that too small?

No. Small technical requirements are where testing matters most, and they are handled on the same terms as large ones.

Tell us the electricians you need

Building or industrial, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer on whether we have men at that level, and our terms in writing.
