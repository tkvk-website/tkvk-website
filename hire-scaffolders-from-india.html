Hire scaffolders from India for the Gulf

Scaffolders from India, each made to erect a real structure at our New Delhi centre and judged on ties, bracing, platforms and whether he reports a problem instead of hiding it. A scaffold that fails inspection stops every trade behind it, so this is tested before anything else. We supply scaffolders to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

What we check when a scaffolder is tested

Erection to a systemStandards, ledgers, transoms and bracing set correctly, working to a method instead of habit.
Ties and baseTie spacing, base plates and sole boards, and knowing when a structure needs more than it was given.
Platforms and accessBoards, toe boards, guard rails and ladders — the first things an inspector looks at.
ReportingWhether he treats an untagged scaffold as unusable and reports a fault rather than patching it quietly.
Your own testYour safety officer can attend in New Delhi or watch by video; the material and the structure are set up for erection, not an interview. How our trade testing works.

Scaffolders for each Gulf country

Scaffolds are inspected by someone other than the builder across the Gulf. What changes is the type of site and how much open-air erection the summer allows.

No open-air work: 12:00–15:00, 15 June–15 September.

Certificates and papers

Where a man holds a recognised scaffolding certificate or an earlier employer’s competency record, it goes with his profile and is described for exactly what it covers. Where he has none, the profile says so and the erection test is the evidence. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. What employers ask about hiring scaffolders from India

Do your scaffolders hold certificates?

Some do, and each is listed for exactly what it covers. Where a man holds none, his profile says so and the practical test stands as the evidence.

Can they work to our scaffolding system?

Tell us the system. If a man has never used it, we tell you instead of assuming he will learn it at height.

Will they report a scaffold that is not right?

That is deliberately tested. A man who quietly patches a problem is more dangerous than a slow one, and the assessment says which he is.

Can you supply a full shutdown gang?

Tell us the size and the date. We will say honestly what tested numbers we can deliver in time.

Can our safety officer test them before we select?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video, watching a real erection.

Can you supply small numbers?

Yes. A few men is a normal requirement and gets the same testing.

Plant or building, your system, numbers, country and the shutdown or start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
