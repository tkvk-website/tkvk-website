Why hospitality staff are tested before shortlisting

Kuwait's food and beverage market is unusually restaurant-led and unusually delivery-led. A large share of covers never sit down at all, which changes the kitchen job: packing accuracy, hold times, and whether food that travels twenty minutes still arrives as intended. That is a different discipline from plating for a table.

The week is concentrated too. Thursday evening through Saturday carries a disproportionate share of trade, and the kitchen that is comfortable on Tuesday is the one that fails on Friday. Staffing and briefing both have to be built around that gap rather than an average week.

Contract catering is the other pillar — staff canteens, camps and institutional feeding at volume, where consistency and hygiene records matter more than presentation.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Kuwait runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Kitchen work for delivery volume

    Packing accuracy, hold times, and whether the dish survives the journey — assessed as a skill rather than assumed.

  2. Hygiene and food safety

    Personal hygiene, temperature discipline, cross-contamination control and cleaning schedules, including the records an inspection asks for.

  3. Working the weekend peak

    Holding sequence and standard through a Thursday-to-Saturday rush that can run at several times the weekday rate.

  4. High-volume contract catering

    Feeding at scale on a fixed schedule, where the same quality has to come out on the four hundredth portion.

  5. Grooming and uniform discipline

    Established before departure rather than corrected on your floor.

Certificates and papers

Hotel management certificates, food handling training and prior property references go with the profile, with a plain note where a candidate has restaurant experience but no hotel experience, or vice versa. Passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared for the Kuwaiti work permit and visa process.

What employers ask about hospitality staff

Do your cooks have delivery-kitchen experience?

Some do, and we tell you which. Cooking for delivery is its own discipline, and a plating cook put on a delivery line without briefing produces food that arrives badly.

Can you supply for weekend-heavy rosters?

Yes, and it is the roster we brief for by default in Kuwait. The important part is that the man is told his week runs Thursday to Saturday while he is still in India.

Do you supply women for housekeeping?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we tell you that at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can you supply for high-volume contract catering?

Yes. Tell us the covers per day and the shift pattern, and we assess against that rather than against restaurant work, which is not the same job.

Tell us how many hospitality staff you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Kuwait.
