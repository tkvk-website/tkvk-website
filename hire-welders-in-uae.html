Why welders are tested before shortlisting

UAE fabrication and fit-out work is handed over on fixed dates and inspected before it is signed off. A weld that fails at that point is not rework you can absorb quietly — it is a delayed handover, and a man you now have to replace from four thousand kilometres away. Everything we do with welders is aimed at that failure happening in New Delhi instead.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the welding test covers

  1. The joint you specify

    Process, position, material, thickness and the code your project works to. A plate welder and a 6G pipe welder are not interchangeable, and we do not present them as if they were.

  2. Visual inspection of the finished weld

    Undercut, porosity, bead profile, start and stop quality, photographed from the angles your inspector uses.

  3. Fit-up and preparation

    Joint prep, gap setting and tacking, because most bad welds are decided before the arc strikes.

  4. Consumables and safety

    Electrode handling and storage, machine settings, screen and glove discipline, and hot-work awareness.

Certificates and papers

Trade certificates, ITI qualifications and previous NDT-passed test records go with the profile where the man holds them, described for exactly what they prove. Where he holds none, we say so and let the test speak.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process, and the job offer you file with MoHRE is matched to what the worker signed here.

What employers ask about welders

Can you supply 6G-qualified pipe welders?

Ask for the specific qualification your work needs and we will tell you honestly whether we have men at that level, and how many. We would rather say no than send a plate welder against a 6G requirement.

Will the worker see the same terms we file with MoHRE?

Yes, and that is the point of the pre-departure briefing. Salary, hours and overtime are explained in his own language before he signs, so the job offer holds no surprises on arrival.

Can our QA man re-test them in the UAE?

Yes, and most employers do. Our test exists so the man reaching your re-test has a real chance of passing it, not to replace your procedure.

What if a welder underperforms on site?

Ask for our replacement terms in writing before you commit rather than assuming them.

Tell us how many welders you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. Use the requirement form, or see all trades we supply to the UAE.
