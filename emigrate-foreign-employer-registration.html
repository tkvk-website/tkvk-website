eMigrate registration for foreign employers, step by step

eMigrate is the Government of India portal that tracks the recruitment of Indian workers for jobs abroad. Before a Gulf company can raise a demand for workers, it registers there as a foreign employer, and the Indian Mission in its country checks the papers. This is what the form asks for, which documents to keep ready, and what happens after approval.

See the steps Send a requirement

Why the registration matters

India requires emigration clearance for workers holding ECR (Emigration Check Required) passports who go to work in any of 18 ECR countries. All six Gulf states are on that list: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. That clearance runs on eMigrate, and the employer has to be registered in the system for its demand, job details and employment contracts to be linked to each worker.

Registration is in the employer’s own name. It is approved by the Indian Embassy or Consulate that covers the place where your company is registered, using documents signed by your authorised signatory.

The registration, step by step

  1. Check the wage and job rules first. The Indian Missions publish job titles and minimum referral wages for each country. Make sure your offer meets them before you start.
  2. Download the request letter. On emigrate.gov.in, the employer section has a “Request Letter to the Indian Mission for FE Registration”, in one version for organisations and one for individual employers. Fill it in on your letterhead, have the authorised signatory sign and stamp it, and keep it for upload. It must be complete, in English and unaltered; missions reject letters that are not.
  3. Open the registration form. Go to Employer → Registration and choose the organisation or individual form.
  4. Enter the basic details. Company name, commercial registration or trade licence number, country, the Indian Mission with jurisdiction, the licence dates and a company email address. The portal sends a one-time password to that email to verify it.
  5. Describe the organisation. Business type, total workforce, how many Indian workers you employ now, and whether any complaints by Indian workers are pending.
  6. Give the office addresses for the operational office and, if different, the registered office.
  7. Add the authorised signatory. Name, designation, nationality, email, passport number, national ID number and residential address.
  8. Upload the documents listed below, as PDFs.
  9. Submit. You receive an application reference number and can download a PDF of the application. Use “Track Application Status” with that number to follow it.
  10. Wait for the Mission’s approval. Once the Indian Mission verifies the papers, the login ID and password are emailed to the registered address. The Consulate General in Dubai says this normally happens within a week of the reference number being issued.

For problems with the form or the one-time password, the eMigrate helpdesk is on +91 11 2688 5021 and helpdesk@emigrate.gov.in.

Documents to keep ready

Every Mission asks for the same core set, then adds local papers. Check the checklist of the Mission that covers you before uploading.

Oman is a good example of local additions: the Embassy of India in Muscat lists the latest commercial registration printout, the Oman Chamber of Commerce and Industry membership certificate, the labour clearance with an English translation, and a declaration by the authorised signatory on letterhead.

After approval: how workers are recruited

Through a registered recruiting agent

For most employers, the next step is to log in, raise a demand, upload the visa or quota papers and choose a recruiting agent registered with India’s Ministry of External Affairs. The portal issues a demand number, and the agent in India handles the remaining steps against it, including emigration clearance. For skilled workers recruited through an agent, the Ministry asks for a demand letter and power of attorney from the employer. Indian Mission guidance in the UAE says recruitment of ten or more workers in a year goes through a registered agent.

Direct recruitment of a few workers

The same UAE guidance allows an employer hiring fewer than ten workers in a year to recruit directly. The Mission attests each worker’s visa, the employer creates the job and the employment contract on eMigrate, the worker buys the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana insurance, and the Protector of Emigrants issues the clearance.

Things that hold files up

  • A trade licence that has expired or expires soon. The registration follows its dates.
  • A signatory whose name does not match the authority document.
  • A designation on the visa that does not match the job on eMigrate.
  • A salary below the referral wage the Mission publishes for that job.

Common questions

Does a foreign employer have to register on eMigrate to hire Indian workers?

Employers outside India who want to recruit Indian workers register on the eMigrate portal, emigrate.gov.in. Workers on ECR passports going to any of the 18 ECR countries, which include all six GCC states, need emigration clearance through eMigrate, and the employer has to be in the system for their demand and contracts to be processed.

What documents does the Indian Mission ask for?

The core set is a request letter generated from the portal and signed on company letterhead, the commercial registration or trade licence, the authorised signatory's passport or ID, and proof of authority for the signatory. Each mission adds local papers: the UAE missions ask for the labour quota page and an address proof, and the Embassy in Oman asks for the Chamber of Commerce certificate and labour clearance.

How long does eMigrate employer registration take?

After you submit, you receive an application reference number and can track it on the portal. The Indian Mission verifies the documents and, once it approves, the login ID and password are emailed to the registered address. The Consulate in Dubai says credentials normally arrive within a week of the reference number.

How long is an eMigrate employer registration valid?

The High Commission of India in Ottawa states that employer registration is valid for five years initially. The UAE form also asks for a 'valid up to' date that follows the expiry of the trade licence, so keep the licence renewed.

Can an employer recruit Indian workers without a recruiting agent?

Indian Mission guidance in the UAE allows direct recruitment, with the visa attested by the Mission, for fewer than ten workers in a year. For larger numbers the employer raises a demand on eMigrate to a recruiting agent registered with India's Ministry of External Affairs.
