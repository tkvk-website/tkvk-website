Hospitality is indoor work, so the summer midday rule hardly applies. What changes is the calendar, the outlets and the local rules on who can work where.

Certificates and papers

Hotel management certificates, food-handling training and earlier property references go with the profile where held. A man who has worked in restaurants but never a hotel is described that way. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. What employers ask about hiring hotel and restaurant staff from India

Do you supply cooks for specific cuisines?

Tell us the cuisine and the outlet. We will not present a camp catering cook as a fine-dining cook.

Will your staff serve alcohol?

We ask every candidate and record the answer, so you know before interview.

Can you staff a property in Makkah or Madinah?

Entry to Makkah is restricted to Muslims. Tell us the property and we say plainly which candidates can lawfully be placed.

Can you mobilise a full opening team?

Tell us headcount, departments and date. The schedule is built on your visa quota and attestation, and we say if the date will not hold.

Do you supply women for housekeeping?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only and says so at the start.

Can one man cover more than one station?

Many can, and it is assessed when you tell us the brigade is small.

Tell us the hotel and restaurant staff you need

Roles, outlet, numbers, country and opening or start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
