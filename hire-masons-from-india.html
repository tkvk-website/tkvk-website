Hire masons from India for the Gulf

Block, plaster and finishing masons from India, each put on a real wall at our New Delhi centre and judged on line, level, finish and the way he handles his mix. Block and plaster ability are reported separately, so your planning is realistic. We supply masons to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the masonry job, not just “mason”

Two masons with the same years of experience can be suited to very different sites. These details decide the shortlist.

Block, plaster or both

Many men are clearly stronger in one. The assessment says which.

Internal or external

Internal plaster runs through the summer midday stop; external blockwork does not.

New build or alteration

Opening up and matching an existing finish exposes a mason faster than a new wall.

Finish standard

If work is judged against a sample panel or a client’s eye, send us a photo of what passes.

Small gangs need men who finish their own work rather than hand it on.

What we check when a mason is tested

Line and levelCourses, plumb, joint thickness and consistency, checked with the instruments your engineer uses.
Plaster finishFlatness, corners and edges — the parts a client walks up to and points at.
Mix and curingRatios, water use and wetting the block. Most Gulf summer cracking is a curing failure, not a skill one.
Output with qualitySpeed is measured on the same panel as quality, because either one on its own misleads.
Your own testYour engineer can attend in New Delhi or watch by video, and can bring your own finish standard to test against. How our trade testing works.

Masons for each Gulf country

A good wall is a good wall everywhere. What changes is the finish the client expects, whether the work is new or alteration, and how the heat treats fresh mortar.

Masons for Saudi ArabiaTypical work: Building and finishing, villas and compounds, large contractor packages, industrial buildings, block and plaster gangs.
No open-air work: 12:00–15:00, 15 June–15 September.
Quantities are large, so one weak mason costs a floor, not a wall; plaster faults show only after he has moved on.
Masons for the UAETypical work: Building and finishing, villas, hotel and retail works, fit-out partitions, refurbishment.
No open-air work: 12:30–15:00, 15 June–15 September.

Masons for OmanTypical work: Building and finishing, villa and compound projects, civil works, industrial buildings, refurbishment.
No open-air work: 12:30–15:30, 1 June–31 August.
Dispersed sites far from suppliers: setting out and material take-off before the day starts are scored.
Masons for QatarTypical work: Building and finishing, internal plaster and partitions, civil packages, housing, industrial buildings.
No open-air work: 10:00–15:30, 1 June–15 September.

Masons for KuwaitTypical work: Building and finishing, repair and alteration, housing, internal plaster, commercial buildings.
No open-air work: 11:00–16:00, 1 June–31 August.

What employers ask about hiring masons from India

Can they do both blockwork and plaster?

Many can, some are clearly better at one. The assessment states which, so you can plan the gang properly.

Our finish standard is strict. Can you test to it?

Send the standard or a photo of an accepted wall, and we test against it and tell you who met it.

Do they understand curing in Gulf heat?

Water discipline and curing are part of the test, because that is where most summer cracking starts.

Do you supply masons with helpers?

Yes. Tell us your gang structure and we will propose a sensible ratio.

Do you supply tilers as masons?

No. Tiling is its own trade; ask for it separately and we will not present a mason as a tiler.

We only need a few masons. Will you take that?

Yes. Small requirements are normal and are handled on the same terms as large ones.

Tell us the masons you need

Block or plaster, internal or external, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
