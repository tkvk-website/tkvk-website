Why heavy drivers are tested before shortlisting

Qatar's heavy transport is short-run and dense: aggregate and readymix feeding construction, trailer work between the Industrial Area and the sites, and industrial transport around Mesaieed and Ras Laffan. Distances are small and the schedules are not forgiving.

Because runs are short, a driver makes many more site entries, reversing moves and load cycles per day than a long-haul man. That is where vehicles get damaged and people get hurt, so that is where we put the assessment.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in the class claimed

    Tipper, mixer, trailer or bus, driven with an assessor present. We grade him for the class he will actually drive, not the broadest class on his licence.

  2. Site entry, tipping and reversing among plant

    Entering and leaving an active site, positioning to tip safely, reversing with banksman signals, and the discipline of not cutting a corner around a barrier. This is the part of Qatari heavy work that causes incidents.

  3. Pre-trip inspection

    Tyres, brakes, lights, hydraulics on a tipper, coupling on a trailer, and the load itself. Watched, not asked about.

  4. Load cycle discipline

    Repeated loads under time pressure — whether he keeps checking, or starts skipping the check by the fourth cycle. The fourth cycle is where the habit shows.

Certificates and papers

Whether an Indian heavy licence converts in Qatar, and what test applies, is decided there and the position changes. Tell us your company's current route and we brief the men on it. What we provide is the verified class, the years held, and a plain statement of what he is ready for — including where he would need supervision at first.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about heavy drivers

Do you supply mixer and tipper drivers specifically?

Yes, and we assess them on that work rather than assuming a trailer driver will adapt. Tipping on uneven ground and handling a rotating drum are their own skills, and we grade them separately.

Can they work to a site banksman and follow signals?

We test it in the assessment — reversing to signals, stopping when the signal stops, and not guessing. A driver who reverses on his own judgement in a live site is the one you will be writing a report about.

Are they fit for long shifts in Qatari summer?

Every man clears a medical at a centre approved for Qatar before travel. Heat tolerance specifically is raised in the pre-departure briefing rather than treated as a tick box.

Can we send our transport manager to test them?

Yes, and we would prefer it. In person at our New Delhi centre or by video, and you can set your own assessment instead of relying only on ours.
