Why light drivers are tested before shortlisting

Qatar is small and dense, which changes what a good light driver looks like. The distances are short, the traffic is not, and the job is usually many drops in a day inside greater Doha rather than long highway runs.

A man who is comfortable on an open road can still be slow and nervous in that environment, so we test for it directly instead of taking a licence at face value.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in a light vehicle

    Saloon, pickup and panel van. Junction judgement, merging in heavy traffic, and reversing into a tight bay without a spotter.

  2. Multi-drop city run

    A route with several stops, judged on sequencing, parking discipline and handover. This is the shape of the job in Doha.

  3. Licence class verified, local process stated

    Indian licence class and validity confirmed, and what your side must arrange in Qatar set out plainly, including the interim arrangement before a permanent licence is issued.

  4. Conduct at the door

    How he presents himself to your client, your guest or your site gate. For hospitality and catering fleets this matters as much as the driving.

What employers ask about light drivers

What happens about his Qatari licence?

There is a local process and an interim arrangement before a permanent licence is issued, and the detail changes. We confirm the current position and tell you what your side arranges, rather than letting the man arrive and find out.

Do you test on the vehicle we actually run?

Yes. Name the class — saloon, pickup, panel van, minibus — and the assessment is run on it.

Are these drivers suitable for guest-facing work?

Some are and some are not, and we say which. For hotel and catering fleets we assess presentation and conduct alongside the driving.

Can you supply a driver and a helper together?

Yes, and for distribution work it is usually the better mix. Tell us the route pattern and we will propose it.

Do you check the driving record?

Police clearance and driving record are part of the file. A record your insurer would reject does not reach your shortlist.
