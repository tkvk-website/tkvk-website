We exist to stop the wrong man boarding the plane

Taj Kaushal Vikas Kendra trains and practically tests Indian tradesmen before they are put in front of a Gulf employer. That is the whole job. Everything else follows from it.

TKVK in brief

A short factual summary, for anyone — or anything — that needs the essentials without reading the whole page.

What TKVK isTaj Kaushal Vikas Kendra (TKVK) is an Indian skill-training and trade-testing organisation. It trains Indian tradesmen and tests them practically before they are presented to a Gulf employer.
What TKVK does not doTKVK is not the licensed recruiting agent. Overseas recruitment and deployment are carried out by Taj HR Services, holder of Government of India recruitment licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by the Ministry of External Affairs.
Who it servesEmployers in the GCC: contractors, facility-management companies, industrial and logistics firms, hotels and retail groups, and households hiring domestic staff.
Where it operatesSix Gulf countries only — Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. No other destinations.
Who it suppliesMale workers only. Where a requirement needs female staff, TKVK says so at the outset rather than accepting the order.
How workers are testedA practical task in the candidate’s own trade, judged by someone who knows that trade. Evidence is sent before the employer interviews, and the employer may run its own test in New Delhi or by video.
Documents preparedPassport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance, certificate attestation and eMigrate PoE clearance. Destination operating licences are issued locally and arranged by the employer.
How to startSend the trade, the number of workers, the salary and terms, and the start date to the requirement form, or email info@tkvk.in.
OfficesNew Delhi (main centre and employer meetings) and Lucknow (sourcing and early-stage training).

Why TKVK is a separate thing

Recruitment agencies are paid to fill a headcount. Testing slows that down, so it usually does not happen — a certificate is glanced at, a man is put on a flight, and whether he can actually do the work becomes your problem four thousand kilometres away.

TKVK was set up so that the testing is somebody’s actual job rather than an optional step in someone else’s. We train, we test, and we tell you plainly what a man can and cannot do — including when the honest answer is that he is not ready.

The overseas recruitment itself is carried out by Taj HR Services, which holds Government of India recruitment licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by the Ministry of External Affairs. You deal with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

Where we work from

New Delhi

Our office and main centre, in New Friends Colony. Employer meetings, candidate assessment and document work run from here. Employers who want to watch a trade test in person come to this address.

Lucknow

Our second training centre, closer to the states much of our candidate intake comes from. Sourcing and early-stage training happen here before candidates come forward for testing.

We work for the six Gulf countries only — Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. We do not handle Europe, Canada, Malaysia or any other destination, and we will say so plainly if you ask.

How we decide what to tell you

A test result is a result

If a man fails, he is told what to fix and retested later. He does not quietly appear on your shortlist because the numbers were short that week.

Papers say what they say

A certificate is sent with the profile and described for exactly what it proves. A domestic wireman is not written up as an industrial electrician, and a plate welder is not offered against a 6G requirement.

No is an answer

When we cannot serve a requirement properly, we say so at the start. A wrong yes costs you months and costs us a client, so it is not worth either of us.

Your test beats ours

Send your technical man, or run the test over video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the material. Our test exists to save your time, not to replace your judgement.

Terms before work

What is charged, what it includes, and what happens if a worker does not perform — in writing before anything starts, not after.

The worker knows too

Salary, hours, overtime and accommodation are explained to the man in his own language before he signs. Workers who understood the terms do not walk off in month two.

Leadership

Who is accountable for the standard, and for the decision to say no.
