Shuttering or finishing? Tell us which

This one decision matters more than anything else in a carpentry requirement. These details refine it.

Shuttering or finishing

Formwork on structure, or fit-out joinery. A good man at one can be poor at the other.

Formwork system

Traditional timber shuttering or a system formwork. Where a man knows only timber, we say so.

Drawings

Whether he must work from formwork or joinery drawings without help.

Workshop machines

If the job is in a joinery shop, which machines he must run safely.

New build or refurbishment

Matching existing work around occupants is harder than new build.

Helpers

Your gang structure, so skilled men are not carrying board and stripping forms.

What we check when a carpenter is tested

FormworkSet to line and level, properly braced and tight enough that grout does not escape — the concrete face is decided here.
JoineryJoint accuracy, edges, fixings and the surface finish a client sees.
Measuring and cutting listsTake-offs and cutting accuracy, because rework in carpentry is pure waste of material and time.
Power toolsGuard use and safe machine handling, where shortcuts cause the worst injuries.

What employers ask about hiring carpenters from India

Do you supply shuttering and finishing carpenters separately?

Yes, and we insist on it. Each profile says plainly which kind of carpenter the man is.

Can they work with system formwork?

Tell us the system. A man who has only done timber shuttering is described that way, not stretched to fit.

Can one man do both shuttering and finishing?

A few genuinely can, and the assessment names them. Most are stronger at one, and we say so.

Do you supply carpenters with helpers?

Yes. Tell us your gang and we will propose a ratio.

Do you cover aluminium and glass fabrication?

Ask separately. That is a different trade and we will not present a carpenter as a fabricator.

Can our engineer test them before selection?

Yes, in New Delhi or by video, with real material and a drawing.

Tell us the carpenters you need

Shuttering or finishing, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
