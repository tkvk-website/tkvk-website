Why electricians are tested before shortlisting

An electrician who cannot read a drawing or terminates untidily does not fail on day one. He fails months later, when a panel trips or an inspection is refused. That is why the practical test matters more than the paperwork.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Kuwait runs through it.

What the electrical test covers

  1. Drawing to wiring

    A schematic, and the man wires it. Whether he reads it unaided is most of the answer.

  2. Termination and panel dressing

    Crimping, lugging, glanding and cable dressing — the most common reason a new electrician is downgraded on site.

  3. Testing and measurement

    Continuity, insulation resistance with a megger, correct multimeter use. A man who guesses instead of measuring is not an electrician.

  4. Isolation and safety

    Proving dead before work, lock-out discipline, and the confidence to refuse an unsafe instruction.

Certificates and papers

ITI Electrician certificates and wireman licences go with the profile where held, described for what they actually prove. A domestic wireman is never presented as an industrial electrician.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process, and attestation is opened as soon as a candidate is confirmed, so it does not hold up your work permit.

What employers ask about electricians

Do you distinguish domestic, commercial and industrial electricians?

Yes. A man who wired villas is not ready for a plant room, and we test to the level your work needs.

Can they work from drawings in English?

The test includes it and the assessment reports per man, not as a blanket claim.

Do you supply electricians with HV experience?

Ask, and we will tell you what we genuinely have. High-voltage work is not something to overstate.

Can our engineer test them by video?

Yes. We arrange the candidates, the panel and the instruments.

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. Use the requirement form, or see all trades we supply to Kuwait.
