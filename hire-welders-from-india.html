Hire welders from India for the Gulf

Arc, MIG and TIG welders from India, each tested on the joint you specify at our New Delhi centre before he reaches your shortlist. You receive photographs of the test piece and a written assessment before you interview. We supply welders to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the weld, not just the trade

“Welder” covers men who will never pass each other’s test. The more of these you give us, the closer the shortlist matches the job.

Process

Arc (stick), MIG or TIG — and whether he must be able to switch between them.

Position

Flat plate, vertical, overhead, or pipe up to 6G. A man tested on plate is not offered against a pipe requirement.

Material and thickness

Carbon steel, stainless or other, and the thickness range he will actually weld.

Code or procedure

The standard your project works to. It decides what we test for and how we judge it.

Shop or site

Fabrication-shop throughput and site improvisation are different skills, and the assessment reports them differently.

Shutdown, permit or remote work

Hot work inside running plant, or a remote posting, changes who is suitable. Say so at the start.

What we check when a welder is tested

The finished weldUndercut, porosity, spatter, bead profile, and the quality of starts and stops. The test piece is photographed from the angles your inspector would use.
Preparation and fit-upHow he preps the joint, sets the gap and tacks — where most bad welds begin before the arc is struck.
Consumables and safetyElectrode storage and handling, machine settings, screen and glove discipline, and hot-work awareness.
ConsistencyWhere your job repeats the same weld for months, consistency is reported separately from his best single weld.
Your own testYour QA man can attend in New Delhi or watch by video, and run his own test instead of, or on top of, ours. How our trade testing works.

Certificates and papers

Where a welder holds a genuine trade certificate, an ITI qualification or a passed test record from a previous employer, it is sent with his profile and described for exactly what it proves — and where he has none, we say so and let the test speak. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process. What employers ask about hiring welders from India

Can you supply 6G-qualified pipe welders?

Tell us the exact qualification and we will say honestly whether we have men at that level, and how many. A plate welder is never put forward against a 6G pipe requirement.

Which welding processes do you cover?

Arc (stick), MIG and TIG, subject to what we can genuinely test for the position, material and thickness you need. Ask with the specific requirement and you get a plain answer.

Will the welders be re-tested when they arrive?

Usually, yes — most Gulf contractors re-qualify welders to their own procedure before they start, and that is normal. Our assessment states the process, position and material each man was tested on, so you can match him to a procedure he can actually pass.

We only need two or three welders. Is that too small?

No. Small requirements are where testing matters most, because there is no room to carry a weak man. They are handled on the same terms as large ones.

Do you supply welders for shutdown and turnaround crews?

Yes. Tell us the window and the numbers. Shutdown work needs men who hold quality under time pressure and treat hot-work permits seriously, and we select and report on that separately from routine fabrication.

Do you attach client approvals such as Aramco or SABIC to candidates?

No. Tell us what your project requires and we will tell you what we actually have, including when the answer is that we do not have it. Client names are not attached to candidates to make them look better.

Tell us the welders you need

Process, position, material, numbers and country. You will get a straight answer on whether we have men at that level, and our terms in writing.
