Why this page exists

Most employer enquiries start with the same twenty questions, and most agency websites answer them with a paragraph that says yes to everything. That wastes your time twice: once reading it, and again when the real answer turns up after a demand letter has been attested.

So the whole question bank is here instead, split by subject. Seven answers on the worker-categories page are a flat no. The cost page carries no price list. The replacement page carries no number of days. Those are the three places an agency is most tempted to invent something, and the omissions are deliberate.
