Visa, documents and compliance

Sixteen questions on paperwork, which is where recruitment timelines are actually lost. Several answers say a step is yours and not ours — that line is worth knowing before you plan around it.

Four documents from you, one sequence from us

From your side the process runs on four documents: the demand letter, the power of attorney, the job order with salary and terms, and the employment contract, attested as your country requires. Send us the drafts before you attest them — reading a draft costs nothing, re-attesting one costs weeks.

From our side it is a sequence rather than a pile: passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance, trade papers and eMigrate clearance, timed so nothing expires while your entry permit is in process.

Visa, document and compliance questions

Permits, visas and clearances

Can you assist with work permits?

Yes, from the India side. The work permit itself is applied for by you in your own country — through Qiwa in Saudi Arabia, MOHRE in the UAE, and the equivalent labour authority in Oman, Qatar, Kuwait or Bahrain. We prepare everything the permit and the visa will ask for from the worker.

Can you assist with employment visas?

Yes. Once your entry permit or visa is issued, we handle the stamping, the emigration clearance and the departure. What we cannot do is apply for the visa on your behalf — that is an employer or sponsor action in your own country.

Can you assist with residence permits after arrival?

The residence permit — iqama, Emirates ID, QID or the local equivalent — is processed by you after arrival, because it depends on your establishment file and the worker's in-country medical. We make sure the man arrives with every document that process will ask for.

Can you assist with medical tests?

Yes, through the approved medical centres required for GCC employment, including the GAMCA/Wafid route where your country requires it. The medical is timed with the rest of the documents so it does not expire while your permit is in process.

Can you assist with document attestation?

Yes. Attestation is where most recruitment timelines are actually lost, so we sequence it rather than leave it to the end. Send us your drafts before you attest them — reading a draft costs nothing, re-attesting one costs weeks.

Can you assist with emigration clearance where required?

Yes. Emigration clearance for an ECR passport holder is granted through the Government of India eMigrate system and is handled by Taj HR Services under its MEA licence. A worker who travels without it where it was required is a problem for you as much as for him.

Documents we need

What documents do you need from the employer?

Four, in the main: the demand letter, the power of attorney, the job order with salary and terms, and the employment contract — attested as your country requires. There is a full page on this site setting out what each one has to say.

What documents do you need from each worker?

Passport with sufficient validity and ECR status, the approved medical, police clearance, trade or ITI certificates where held, previous employment records where they exist, photographs and the eMigrate PoE clearance. The profile you receive shows the status of each.

Do you require a demand letter?

Yes, and it is the document the whole process hangs on. It must state the trades, the numbers, the salary and the terms exactly as you intend them, because everything downstream — visa category, contract, briefing — is built from it.

Do you require a power of attorney?

Yes, attested, authorising the licensed recruiting agent to act for you in the recruitment. It is a standard requirement of the Indian emigration process rather than a condition we impose.

Contracts, job titles and compliance

Do you require an employment contract?

Yes, and the candidate must see it before he signs. A contract that matches the demand letter and the verbal offer is the simplest protection against a worker leaving in the first months, which is the expensive failure in this business.

Do you require a job description and salary details?

Yes, per category. A vague job description produces the wrong shortlist, and a salary stated to us but not to the candidate produces a man who arrives and then argues. Both should be identical in the demand letter and the contract.

Can you help prepare offer letters?

Yes, we will review your draft and tell you what is missing or ambiguous before it goes to a candidate. The offer letter is the document the man actually remembers, so it is worth getting right.

Can you help prepare employment contracts?

We review and comment; we do not draft your contract for you, and we are not your legal advisers. Where a clause will cause a problem at the emigration stage or on arrival, we will point at it plainly.

Can you check whether the proposed job title is suitable for the visa process?

Yes, and this is worth asking before you apply rather than after. A job title that does not match the visa category, or does not match the trade the man was tested in, is one of the quieter reasons an application is rejected or a worker is stopped.

Can you explain employer compliance requirements?

We can set out what the Indian side requires of you — attested demand letter, power of attorney, job order and contract — and what the worker must hold. Compliance inside your own country, including quota, nationalisation bands and labour-file standing, is yours and your local consultant's, not ours.
