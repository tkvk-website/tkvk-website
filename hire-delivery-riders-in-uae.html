Why delivery riders are tested before shortlisting

Delivery is the one role where the employer carries the road risk directly. A rider who treats a delivery window as a race costs you an accident, an insurance claim and a public incident. Riding discipline matters more than speed.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the assessment covers

  1. Riding assessment

    Control, observation, lane discipline and behaviour at junctions, watched by someone competent to judge it.

  2. Road awareness and defensive riding

    Following distance, anticipating other drivers, and the judgement to slow down when a delivery time is at risk.

  3. Licence verification

    Indian licence class and validity are checked and stated, along with what your side must arrange for UAE licensing.

  4. App and navigation basics

    Working with a delivery app, reading a map, and reporting a problem instead of disappearing with an order.

Certificates and papers

Indian two-wheeler licences are verified for class and validity and sent with the profile. Where UAE licence conversion or local testing will be needed, we say so upfront rather than leaving you to discover it.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your entry permit is in process.

What employers ask about delivery riders

Will their Indian licence be valid in the UAE?

Conversion and local testing requirements apply and they change. We prepare the documents and tell you what your side must arrange.

Do you supply riders with prior Gulf riding experience?

Sometimes, and we state it per man from his record rather than as a general claim.

How do you screen for safe riding rather than fast riding?

The assessment judges discipline and observation, and a rider who is fast but reckless fails it.

Can you supply riders in bulk for a platform launch?

Yes, with a realistic schedule. Tell us the numbers and the date and we will tell you what is achievable.

Tell us how many delivery riders you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to the UAE.
