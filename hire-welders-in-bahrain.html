Why welders are tested before shortlisting

Bahraini employers usually hire welders in small, specific numbers — two or three for a workshop, a handful for a maintenance contract. That makes one weak man a large share of the crew, and it makes the test matter more here than it does on a hundred-man mobilisation.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Bahrain runs through it.

What the welding test covers

  1. The joint you specify

    Process, position, material, thickness and the code your project works to. A plate welder and a 6G pipe welder are not interchangeable, and we do not present them as if they were.

  2. Visual inspection of the finished weld

    Undercut, porosity, bead profile, start and stop quality, photographed from the angles your inspector uses.

  3. Fit-up and preparation

    Joint prep, gap setting and tacking, because most bad welds are decided before the arc strikes.

  4. Consumables and safety

    Electrode handling and storage, machine settings, screen and glove discipline, and hot-work awareness.

Certificates and papers

Trade certificates, ITI qualifications and previous NDT-passed test records go with the profile where the man holds them, described for exactly what they prove. Where he holds none, we say so and let the test speak.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process, and documents are prepared in the form LMRA processing expects.

What employers ask about welders

We only need two or three welders. Is that too small?

No. Small requirements are where testing matters most, because there is no room to carry a weak man. We handle them on the same terms as large ones.

Can we test them before committing?

Yes, in person at our New Delhi centre or by video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the consumables.

Can you supply 6G-qualified pipe welders?

Ask for the specific qualification and we will tell you honestly what we have. We would rather say no than overstate it.

What are your commercial terms?

Ask and we will send them in writing before anything starts — what is charged, what it includes, and what happens if a worker does not perform.

Tell us how many welders you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. Use the requirement form, or see all trades we supply to Bahrain.
