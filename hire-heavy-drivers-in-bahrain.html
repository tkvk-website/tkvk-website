Why heavy drivers are tested before shortlisting

Bahrain's heavy work is short-distance and varied. A driver may run aggregate in the morning, a flatbed load in the afternoon and a delivery across the island after that, and some loads continue on towards the Eastern Province.

Short runs mean far more manoeuvring per kilometre than long-haul work. Most of the damage a transport company pays for in Bahrain happens in yards and at delivery points, not on the highway, so that is what we weight the assessment towards.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Bahrain runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in the class claimed

    Trailer, tipper or flatbed, driven with an assessor present. We record what he can actually handle rather than the widest class printed on his licence.

  2. Yard manoeuvring and tight delivery points

    Reversing into constrained bays, positioning on uneven ground, and working where there is no room for a second attempt. This is where Bahrain's heavy work actually costs money.

  3. Pre-trip inspection and load security

    Tyres, brakes, lights, coupling, chains and straps on a flatbed. Load security matters more where loads change type through the day.

  4. Paperwork and handover discipline

    Delivery notes, weighbridge slips and clean handovers. Where a load moves on beyond Bahrain, a sloppy record follows it and becomes somebody's argument later.

Certificates and papers

Whether an Indian heavy licence converts in Bahrain, and on what terms, is decided there and changes over time. Tell us your company's current practice and we brief the men before travel. From us you get the verified class, the years held, and an honest note on what the man is not yet ready to drive.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about heavy drivers

Our loads sometimes continue across the causeway. Does that change the driver you send?

It changes the paperwork discipline required more than the driving, and we say plainly that border and permit arrangements are your side, not ours. We assess whether the man keeps his documents in order, because that is the part he controls.

We need drivers who also help load and secure.

Common here, and we test it — chains, straps and load distribution on a flatbed, not just the driving. Say so in the requirement and it becomes part of the assessment.

Can you supply a small number?

Yes, and they get the same assessment as a large batch. We will tell you plainly if a very small number pushes the cost per man up for you.

What if a driver turns out to be weaker than the report suggested?

Tell us and we will look at what we got wrong, because that is our error to own. Ask for our replacement terms and you will get them in writing before you commit — the window, the conditions, and what each side is responsible for.
