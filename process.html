From your requirement to men on your site

Eight stages, three parties, and one calendar. This page sets out who does what and where the time actually goes, so you can plan against it instead of being told “soon” every fortnight.

Who does what

Three parties, and it is worth being precise about them, because agencies that blur this are usually hiding something.

You

Set the requirement and the terms, issue and attest the four documents, interview and select, and hold your visa quota open. Nothing below can move faster than these.

TKVK

The eight stages

  1. You send the requirement

    The trade, how many men in each, the salary and terms, accommodation and food, contract length, and the date you need them on site. If your project works to a code or a standard, say so now — it changes what we test for. You get our written terms before any work begins.

  2. Your four documents go for attestation

    The demand letter, power of attorney, job order and guarantee letter, on your letterhead, attested by your chamber of commerce and then by the Indian mission in your country. Send us the drafts before you attest them; reading them takes an afternoon and a rejection costs weeks. This stage starts immediately and runs alongside everything below.

  3. We source and trade-test

    Candidates come from our own trained pool and from sourcing across Indian states, then each one does the actual work under test — the welder welds, the electrician wires and meggers a board, the operator runs the machine. Only men who pass go forward, and a failure is written down rather than quietly reappearing later.

  4. You shortlist and interview

    You receive the assessment sheets and test evidence before any interview is scheduled, so your technical man can screen without travelling. Interviews then run at our New Delhi centre or by video in your timezone. You are welcome to run your own test instead of, or on top of, ours.

  5. Medical and police clearance

    Selected men go to a medical centre approved for your country, and police clearance is obtained. A man who fails the medical is out of the batch — this is the stage where a batch most often shrinks, and it is better that it shrinks here than at your gate.

  6. The country stage

    This is the part that differs most: Musaned for Saudi Arabia, a MoHRE offer signed in India for the UAE, labour clearance for Oman, the Qatar Visa Centre in India, a work permit against your quota for Kuwait, an LMRA visa against your named entity for Bahrain. Taj HR Services runs this as the licensed agent, against your attested documents.

  7. eMigrate and PoE clearance

    Where the worker holds an ECR passport, the file goes to the Protector of Emigrants through eMigrate before he can travel. This is an Indian legal requirement, not a formality to be worked around, and it is one reason the licence number matters — you can verify it yourself on the portal.

  8. Briefing, ticketing, arrival

    Pre-departure briefing on your contract, your site rules and what is expected of him, then travel arranged to the date you asked for. You get the flight details and the names, and if anything threatens the date you hear it from us before it becomes your problem.

What overlaps and what does not

Most of the time lost in Gulf recruitment is lost by running in sequence things that could have run together.

Runs in parallel

Attestation of your documents; sourcing and trade testing. These two tracks do not depend on each other, and starting attestation late is the single most common way a date is missed.

Runs in sequence

Selection → medical → country visa stage → PoE clearance → travel. Each of these needs the one before it to be finished, so time lost early is not recoverable later.

What you control

How quickly the four documents are issued and attested, how quickly selections are confirmed after interviews, and whether the terms in the job order are the terms you will actually pay.

What we control

The quality and honesty of the testing, the completeness of every file before it is submitted, and telling you the truth about the date while there is still time to act on it.

Where the calendar is actually lost

Four things cause most missed dates. None of them are mysterious, and all of them are easier to handle early than late.

A document sent back at attestation

A job title that does not match the job order, a salary stated two different ways, an unsigned page. We read your drafts against what your country currently asks for, rather than against a template that was right last year.

A medical failure late in the batch

Nothing removes this risk entirely. What reduces it is not waiting until the last week to send men for the medical, so there is room to replace from the tested pool.

Visa quota or portal issues at your end

Your establishment file, quota or block visa position is yours to hold open. We will tell you what we are seeing from the Indian side, but we cannot fix a quota problem for you.

A candidate who disappears

Men change their minds, usually for a family reason. We replace from men who have passed the same test, not from whoever is available that week.

What you get while it runs

Assessment sheets and test photographs or video, sent before you are asked to schedule anything.

What employers ask about the process

How long does the whole thing take?

Honestly: it depends on your country and on how fast your documents clear attestation, and any agency that answers with a fixed number before seeing your requirement is guessing. What we will do is give you a dated plan against your actual job order once the requirement is confirmed, and tell you the same week if anything threatens it.

Which stage usually causes the delay?

Attestation of your own documents, more often than anything on the Indian side. A wrong job title, a salary that contradicts the job order, or a missing signature sends the set back and the clock restarts. That is why we ask to read your drafts before you attest them.

Can sourcing start before our documents are attested?

Yes, and it should. Sourcing and trade testing run in parallel with attestation. What cannot start early is the visa stage, because that needs the attested set.

What do we have to do at each stage?

Four things, really: send the requirement and terms, issue and attest the four documents, interview and select, and confirm the arrival date. Everything between those is ours to run and report on.

Who is legally recruiting the workers?

Taj HR Services, holder of Government of India recruitment licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. TKVK trains and tests. Deployment is the licensed agent’s job and we do not blur the two.

What happens if a selected man drops out before travel?

It happens, usually at the medical or for a family reason. We tell you the day we know, and go back to the tested pool rather than putting forward someone who has not been through the same test.
