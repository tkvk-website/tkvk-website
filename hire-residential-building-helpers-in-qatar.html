Why accommodation helpers are assessed before shortlisting

A large share of Qatar's accommodation work is worker housing at scale — blocks with hundreds of men, shared kitchens and ablution areas, inspected against standards that are enforced.

At that density, hygiene is not cosmetic. An ablution block cleaned badly becomes an inspection finding and then a compliance problem, so we test the routine and the chemical handling rather than assuming a man knows.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Hygiene routine in shared facilities

    Ablution areas, shared kitchens and corridors worked to a set sequence and frequency. We set the routine, leave the man, and check what was actually done.

  2. Chemical safety and dilution

    Correct products, correct strength, no mixing, and safe storage. In shared facilities this is a safety matter, not a cost matter, and we grade it accordingly.

  3. Inspection readiness

    Whether the man understands that the standard has to hold on a Tuesday afternoon, not only before a walkthrough. We check an area unannounced later in the session.

  4. Conduct in shared accommodation

    Handling hundreds of residents, disputes over mess, and reporting rather than arguing. Accommodation blocks fail on conduct more often than on cleaning.

Papers and what we will not promise

Accommodation work carries no real trade certificate and we do not claim one. We send the preparation record — routine, chemical handling, unannounced spot check and conduct observations — with the verified work history and a plain note on what we could not confirm.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about building helpers

We are inspected regularly. Will the standard hold between inspections?

That is exactly what the unannounced check in our assessment is for. We report what an area looked like when the man did not know it would be checked, which is the only useful version of that answer.

Can you supply a supervisor as well as cleaners?

Yes, and we assess supervisors separately — the ability to allocate, to check, and to report a problem upward rather than absorb it. Tell us the team shape you need.

Are they fit for the summer workload?

Every man clears a medical at a centre approved for Qatar before travel, and heat is covered honestly in the pre-departure briefing rather than treated as a formality.

Can we inspect the preparation ourselves?

Yes — at our New Delhi centre in person or by video, and you are welcome to set your own practical check instead of relying only on ours.

Tell us how many building helpers you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Qatar.
