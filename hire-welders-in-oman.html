Why welders are tested before shortlisting

Omani work leans heavily on oil and gas services, tank farms and workshop fabrication, where a weld either passes inspection or costs you a re-test and a delay. We make the discovery happen in New Delhi rather than at your inspection point.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Oman runs through it.

What the welding test covers

  1. The joint you specify

    Process, position, material, thickness and the code your work follows. A plate welder and a 6G pipe welder are not interchangeable, and we do not present them as if they were.

  2. Visual inspection of the finished weld

    Undercut, porosity, bead profile, start and stop quality, photographed from the angles your inspector uses.

  3. Fit-up and preparation

    Joint prep, gap setting and tacking, because most bad welds are decided before the arc strikes.

  4. Consumables and safety

    Electrode handling and storage, machine settings, and hot-work discipline.

Certificates and papers

Trade certificates, ITI qualifications and previous test records go with the profile where the man holds them, described for exactly what they prove. Where he holds none, we say so and let the test speak.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about welders

Do you supply welders for shutdown crews in Oman?

Tell us the window and the numbers. Shutdown work needs men who hold quality under time pressure, and we select and test for that differently from routine fabrication.

Can our inspector re-test them on arrival?

Yes, and most employers do. Our test exists so the man reaching your re-test has a real chance of passing it.

What welding processes do you cover?

Arc, MIG and TIG, subject to what we can genuinely test for the position you need. Ask for the specific requirement and we will answer plainly.

What happens if a welder underperforms on site?

Ask for our replacement terms in writing before you commit rather than assuming them.

Tell us how many welders you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to Oman.
