Why light drivers are tested before shortlisting

Bahrain is small enough that a light driver covers the island in a morning — and then, often, crosses the King Fahd Causeway into the Saudi Eastern Province in the afternoon. That crossing changes the job: different rules, different traffic, different paperwork, and a queue that rewards a patient driver and punishes a careless one.

So the assessment covers the ordinary distribution work and the discipline the crossing needs, and we state per man whether he is suited to it.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Bahrain runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in a light vehicle

    Saloon, pickup and panel van. Lane discipline, junction judgement, and reversing loaded into a tight bay without a spotter.

  2. Multi-drop island run

    A route with several stops judged on sequencing, parking without blocking, and clean handover. This is most of the work in Bahrain.

  3. Licence class verified, local process stated

    Indian licence class and validity confirmed, and what your side must arrange for the Bahraini licence set out plainly.

  4. Document and crossing discipline

    Keeping vehicle and load papers in order and behaving predictably at a border queue. For fleets that run the causeway this decides whether a load arrives today or tomorrow.

What employers ask about light drivers

Do your drivers handle causeway runs?

Some are suited to it and some are not. We assess document discipline and behaviour in a queue alongside the driving, and we state per man whether we would put him on that run.

What about the Bahraini licence?

There is a local process and the requirements change. We confirm the current position and set out what your side must arrange rather than leaving the man to find out.

Can he drive a van as well as a car?

Tested on the class you name. Loaded panel van handling is assessed separately from a saloon.

Do you supply drivers for hospitality work?

Yes, and for guest-facing roles we assess presentation and conduct as well as driving, then say plainly which men suit it.

Is the driving record checked?

Police clearance and driving record are part of the file. A record your insurer would reject does not reach your shortlist.

Tell us what your Bahrain fleet runs

Send the vehicle classes, the routes and the number of men. We will tell you how we would assess for it and where the licence stands in Bahrain.
