Why forklift operators are tested before shortlisting

The UAE is where the licence question bites hardest, and it is worth being precise about it. A light forklift is driven on a UAE licence issued by the RTA, the minimum age is 20, and the operator completes local training and passes local tests before he holds it. A valid foreign licence in the same category can cut the required training hours — commonly from twenty down to fifteen or ten — but it does not remove the requirement.

That means the honest question is not just whether he can operate. It is how many weeks of local training and testing sit between his arrival and his first legal shift, and who is paying for them. We put that on the table at the start.

The work itself is dense. Jebel Ali, the free zones and the logistics parks run high-throughput racked warehouses with pedestrians, pickers and other trucks in the same aisle, and the operator who is safe there is a different hire from one who has only loaded flatbeds in a yard.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the operator test covers

  1. Load handling and stability

    Fork positioning, tilt, load centre and the judgement that keeps the load on the forks through a turn.

  2. Narrow-aisle and racked work

    Placing and retrieving at height in a live aisle, mast discipline and lowering before travelling.

  3. Pedestrian safety in a busy warehouse

    Blind corners, horn discipline, speed with pickers on foot, and stopping rather than assuming.

  4. Pre-shift inspection

    Forks, chains, hydraulics, tyres, battery or LPG, and whether a defect gets reported or ridden out.

  5. Truck types and real hours

    Counterbalance, reach, stacker, order picker and powered pallet truck recorded separately, with honest hours against each.

The licence, stated plainly

One thing we will not let you discover late: an Indian forklift certificate is not a Gulf operating licence. Every GCC country issues its own, after its own testing, and the man cannot lawfully run a truck on your site until that is done. What an Indian licence in the same category usually buys you is a shorter local training requirement, not an exemption. So we test him here so you know he can actually operate, we send his Indian papers verified rather than described, and we tell you in writing that the local licence sits on your side of the line. An agency that tells you otherwise is selling you a problem that surfaces in week three.

What employers ask about forklift operators

Will his Indian forklift licence work in the UAE?

Not on its own. A UAE licence is issued by the RTA after local training and testing, with a minimum age of 20. A valid Indian licence in the same category can reduce the training hours required rather than remove the requirement. We verify and send his papers; the local licence is arranged on your side.

How long before he can legally operate?

That depends on the training hours he ends up needing and on test scheduling, both of which sit with the local institute rather than with us. We will not invent a number. What we can do is make sure his Indian licence category is verified, because that is the part that shortens it.

Do you supply women operators?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can you supply reach truck operators specifically?

Tell us the truck and the aisle width. We will confirm who has genuine hours on that machine, and we will say so when nobody in the pool does.
