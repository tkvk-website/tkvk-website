Why security guards are prepared before shortlisting

Security is judged on the days nothing happens. A guard who cannot write a clear incident report, or who leaves a post unmanned, creates the gap that an incident later walks through. That is what preparation has to address.

The UAE is the one Gulf market where the licensing step, not the man, usually decides when a guard can stand a post. Employers who plan around the man and discover the licence afterwards lose weeks they had already promised to a client.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the preparation covers

Access control and identity checks

Gate procedure, visitor and vehicle logs, and the confidence to stop someone who should not pass.

Patrol discipline

Route, timing, variation and reporting. A predictable patrol is barely a patrol at all.

Incident response and reporting

What to do first, who to call, and how to write a report that is usable afterwards.

Conduct and de-escalation

Handling an argument without escalating it, and knowing the limits of a guard's authority.

Certificates and papers

Ex-servicemen records and prior security service references go with the profile where held, along with police clearance and full medical status. Formal security certification is uncommon in India and we do not claim it where it does not exist.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process, and the job offer you file with MoHRE is matched to what the worker signed here.

The licensing step that decides your start date

A guard working in Dubai needs an individual SIRA security guard card. It is issued to the person, not to the employer, and the route to it runs through a valid UAE residence visa, a SIRA-approved training course and a SIRA examination — so the card cannot be obtained before the man arrives and his residency is in place. Dubai is regulated by SIRA under Dubai Police; Abu Dhabi has its own licensing under Abu Dhabi Police, and the other emirates have their own arrangements — tell us which emirate and we will prepare the file for it. Plan the gap between arrival and first shift rather than discovering it. Separately, gate and perimeter posts are outdoor work, and the summer ban on work in direct sun runs 12:30 to 15:00 from 15 June to 15 September.

What employers ask about security guards

Can a guard start work as soon as he lands?

No, and anyone who tells you otherwise is guessing. The SIRA card requires residency, approved training and an examination, all after arrival. Build that period into the plan and the mobilisation stops being a surprise.

Do you arrange the SIRA training and card?

No. It is a UAE-side process that needs the man to be in the country on your residency, and it runs through your licensed security company. What we do is make sure nothing in his Indian papers, police clearance or medical delays it.

Can you supply guards who speak English?

Spoken English is graded honestly on a simple scale rather than claimed for everyone. For reception-facing and supervisory-adjacent posts it is worth filtering on, and we will tell you how deep that part of the pool is.

Do you supply ex-servicemen?

Some of the pool are ex-servicemen and their records go with the profile. We do not describe a man as ex-service without the discharge paper to show for it.
