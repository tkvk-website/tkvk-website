Why heavy drivers are tested before shortlisting

Heavy work in the UAE is container haulage out of the ports, tipper work feeding construction, and bus transport moving large workforces — often across emirates in the same shift, in heavy traffic, on a tight slot.

The penalty system makes the employer pay for the driver's habits. Black points, impounded vehicles and fines sit on the company, and a fleet carrying two impatient drivers loses money quietly. Habits are what we assess; a licence only proves he was once allowed to drive.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in the class claimed

    Articulated, tipper or bus, driven with an assessor present. A man who claims trailer and handles it like a rigid truck is recorded as a rigid-truck driver, whatever his licence says.

  2. Coupling, container and load security

    Coupling and uncoupling, twist locks where relevant, load restraint and weight distribution. Most of what goes wrong in container haulage goes wrong before the vehicle moves.

  3. Pre-trip inspection

    Tyres, brakes, lights, coupling, fluids and spare. Performed properly or performed for the assessor — we record which one it was.

  4. Traffic discipline and cost awareness

    Lane discipline on multi-lane interchanges, following distance, phone and speed discipline. In this market that is a direct cost line, not a soft skill, so we treat it as a graded item.

Certificates and papers

Whether an Indian heavy licence converts in the UAE, for which class and nationality, is set by the authorities and changes. Tell us what your company normally does and we brief the men accordingly. From us you get the licence class, the years held, and an honest statement of what the man is ready to drive on day one.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about heavy drivers

Can they handle container work from the ports?

Tell us that is the job and we assess coupling, twist locks and yard manoeuvring specifically, rather than sending a general trailer driver and hoping. Yard work is where inexperienced men damage equipment.

Our drivers cross emirates daily. Does that matter?

It affects fatigue and schedule discipline more than driving skill, and we brief the men on the actual pattern before they fly. A driver surprised by his real roster does not last.

Do you supply bus drivers for labour transport?

Yes, and we assess them separately from cargo drivers. Carrying fifty men is a different job from carrying a container, and smoothness and patience matter more than speed.

What happens to the fines a new driver collects?

That is between you and your driver, and we will not pretend to control it. What we can do is grade discipline honestly before you hire, so you are choosing with the information rather than finding out from the fines.
