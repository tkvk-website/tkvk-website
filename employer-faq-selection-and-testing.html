Testing, interviews and verification

Twenty-five questions on how a candidate reaches your shortlist. The short version: he welds, wires or lays the work in New Delhi first, and you see the result before you see him.

Send your requirement All employer questions

Evidence first, interview second

The sequence is deliberate. Every candidate is practically tested at our New Delhi centre, and the assessment sheet, photographs or video of the work, and the document status reach you before the interview. You then interview only the men you want to.

You can also test them yourself, in person at the centre or over video, against your own criteria — send your WPS, your checklist or a photograph of an accepted piece of work and we will test to that standard instead of ours.

Selection and testing questions

Experience and language

Do candidates have previous GCC experience?

Many do and many do not, and we state it per man rather than as a claim about the pool. Returning Gulf hands need less briefing; first-time men are often hungrier and cheaper. The assessment sheet lets you choose on facts.

Do candidates have experience in Saudi Arabia?

Some do, with the iqama history and previous employer details recorded where they exist. If prior Saudi experience is a hard requirement, say so at enquiry stage and the shortlist will be filtered on it from the start.

Do candidates have experience in the UAE?

Some do. UAE returnees are common in the finishing trades and in facility management in particular. We list the emirate, the employer and the period where the man can evidence it, and leave it blank where he cannot.

Do candidates have experience in Oman, Qatar, Kuwait or Bahrain?

Yes, across all four, in varying numbers by trade. Tell us which country's experience matters to you and we will tell you honestly how deep that part of the pool is at the time you ask.

Can you provide workers with a specific number of years of experience?

Yes, as a filter on the shortlist. But be careful what you are buying: years are self-reported and a practical test is not. Where the two disagree, we report both and tell you which one we believe.

Can you provide candidates with English-speaking ability?

Yes, and we grade it honestly on a simple scale rather than calling everyone fluent. For supervisory-adjacent roles, retail and hospitality we test spoken English as part of the assessment.

Can you provide candidates with Arabic-speaking ability?

A minority speak working Arabic, usually men who have already worked in the Gulf. It is a genuine filter but a narrow one, so tell us early if it is a requirement rather than a preference.

Can you provide an Arabic-speaking supervisor?

Sometimes, and only where the man genuinely has both the trade standing and the language. We will not promote a tradesman to supervisor on paper to fill a line on your demand letter.

Can you provide an English-speaking supervisor?

More often than Arabic. Where a man has run a gang and speaks working English, he is flagged for you — with the assessment making clear that this is site experience, not a supervisory qualification.

Testing and interviews

Do you conduct trade tests before selection?

Always, and before you see the shortlist rather than after. That sequence is the point of TKVK: you receive the assessment, the photographs of the work and the document status first, and interview only the men you want to.

Can we send our own trade-test criteria?

Yes, and we prefer it. Send your WPS, your checklist or a photograph of an accepted piece of work and we will test against that standard, then tell you which men met it and which did not.

Can we conduct online interviews?

Yes. We set up the candidate with the material and the work in frame so you are watching him work rather than answer questions. Tell us the trade in advance so the bench is ready.

Can we conduct in-person interviews?

Yes. Employers are welcome at the New Delhi centre and many prefer it. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the consumables for the trades you name.

Can we conduct interviews in India?

Yes, that is the normal arrangement — at our own centre in New Delhi. If you need a larger venue for a mass selection, tell us the numbers and the dates and we will arrange it.

Can you arrange a trade-test centre?

We are one. The testing happens at our own centre in New Delhi rather than at a hired yard, which is why an employer can walk in and watch a candidate work on any working day.

Can you send trade-test videos?

Yes, video of the actual work is part of what goes with the assessment. A video of a man welding is worth more than a paragraph describing him as an experienced welder, which is why we send it unasked.

Documents, verification and medicals

Can you provide CVs before selection?

Yes, and with the test assessment attached to each one. A CV on its own is the weakest document in this industry; a CV with a graded practical test beside it is a different thing.

Can you provide passport copies before selection?

Yes, with the pages you need for the visa application and the ECR status visible. We share only what the visa process requires and nothing more, and we do not post candidate documents on any public page.

Can you verify employment experience?

Where there is a previous employer record, a release letter or a Gulf iqama history, we check it and report what it says. Where a man's claim cannot be evidenced, we mark it as unverified rather than repeating it as fact.

Can you verify education and technical certificates?

ITI and trade certificates go with the profile and are described for exactly what they cover. Where a certificate looks doubtful we say so, and the practical test is what settles the question either way.

Can you verify police-clearance certificates?

A police clearance certificate is obtained for every candidate as part of the standard document set, and it is a genuine issued document rather than something we produce. Its status is shown on the profile before you select.

Can you arrange medical fitness tests?

Yes, through the approved medical centres required for GCC employment, including GAMCA/Wafid where the destination requires it. The medical is sequenced with the other documents so nothing expires while your entry permit is in process.

Can you provide workers with safety training?

Safety behaviour is assessed inside every trade test — isolation before electrical work, guard use on machines, reporting rather than improvising on a scaffold. Formal certificated safety courses are a separate thing; tell us which certificate you need and we will say whether we can arrange it.

Can you provide workers with specific site certifications?

Where a man already holds one — a scaffolding ticket, a plant operator card, a previous client's competency record — it goes with the profile and is described for exactly what it covers. We do not issue or arrange client-specific site certifications ourselves.

Can you provide workers with valid driving licences?

Yes, with the Indian licence class and validity stated per man. Be aware that an Indian licence is not directly exchangeable in every GCC state — several require a local driving test after arrival — so build the conversion period into your plan.
