How to hire workers from India for the Gulf

A Gulf employer hires Indian workers through a licensed Indian recruiting agent: you set the terms and attest four documents, the men are trade-tested and interviewed in India, and your country’s permit and India’s emigration clearance follow. This page puts the whole route for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain in one place.

Decide these seven things before you contact anyone

Every delay we see traces back to something that was not decided at the start. Settle these and any honest agency can give you a real answer in its first reply.

1. The exact trade

“6G pipe welder on carbon steel”, not “welder”. If the work follows a code or standard, name it — it changes what is tested.

2. How many in each trade

Numbers per category, and whether a first batch can travel ahead of the rest.

3. The basic salary

You set it, per category. It must read the same in the demand letter, the job order and the contract the worker signs.

4. Accommodation, food and transport

Provided, paid as an allowance, or neither — stated plainly. This is the commonest cause of a worker leaving in the first months.

5. Contract length and hours

Duration, working hours and overtime rate, written into the contract before the man signs.

6. The date you need them on site

Everything is planned backwards from this date, and you are told at the start whether it holds.

7. Your permit position

The visa quota or labour approval in your own country. That stage is yours, and nothing in India can move faster than it.

What each Gulf country asks of the employer

The Indian side is the same for all six countries. What changes is the permit route in your country and the step the worker completes in India before he flies.

Saudi ArabiaYour documents are attested through the Saudi chamber and the Indian mission. The work visa is issued against your establishment and the file and contract are registered on the Saudi platforms (Musaned, Qiwa). Residence: iqama, after arrival.
United Arab EmiratesYou file the job offer with MoHRE and the worker signs it in India before departure; the later contract must match it. Entry permit against your establishment, then medical and Emirates ID after arrival. Wages paid through WPS.
OmanThe Ministry of Labour clears the categories you are approved for, and the employment visa is issued on that clearance. Residence and labour card formalities follow on arrival.
QatarThe worker completes his medical, biometrics and contract signing at a Qatar Visa Centre in India. Statutory minimum wage applies, with food and accommodation allowances where you do not provide them. Residence: QID.
KuwaitWork permits are issued against the manpower authorisation you hold. Educational and trade certificates generally need attestation before the permit stage, so it is opened as soon as a man is confirmed.
BahrainWork visas are issued by the LMRA and tied to your establishment, for the categories you are permitted to recruit.
All sixEach is an ECR-notified country: a worker on an ECR passport needs Protector of Emigrants clearance on eMigrate before departure. A GCC-approved medical in India and police clearance come first.

What employers ask about hiring from India

Do I need an Indian recruitment agency to hire workers from India?

For workers on ECR passports, yes in practice: they need emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants through eMigrate before they can fly, and that file is submitted by a recruiting agent registered with India's Ministry of External Affairs. Here that agent is Taj HR Services, licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, which you can check on eMigrate yourself.

Can I test and interview the workers before I select them?

Yes. Every candidate is practically tested in his trade first, and you receive the assessment and photographs before any interview is booked. Interviews run at the New Delhi centre or by video in your timezone, and you can run your own test instead of, or on top of, ours.

Is there a minimum number of workers?

No fixed minimum. Requirements of three or four men are normal, especially for Bahrain and Oman. For large numbers the limit is usually paperwork and medical queues rather than candidates, so tell us the trade, country and date and you will get an honest answer.

Do you supply female workers?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only, in every category. If your requirement needs women, we say so at the start rather than accept it.

Which Gulf countries can I hire for?

Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. No other destinations.

Who pays the cost of recruitment?

The employer. The work permit and visa sit on the employer's side in every GCC state, and the recruitment model is employer-funded: the worker is not charged for the job. The fee is quoted in writing against your actual requirement, with inclusions and exclusions listed.
