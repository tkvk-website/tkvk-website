Why packing helpers are assessed before shortlisting

A large share of packing work in the UAE is order packing for e-commerce and re-export rather than a single product on a line. The man is picking several different items into one parcel, and every mistake reaches a customer with a photo and a refund.

Multi-item packing is a different skill from repetitive line packing, and men who are excellent at one are often poor at the other. We test the one you are actually hiring for.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Multi-item order accuracy

    Packing several different SKUs into one order against a pick list, with errors counted. Wrong item, missing item and extra item are recorded separately, because they cost you different amounts.

  2. Fragile handling and protective packing

    Wrapping, void fill and how he places a heavy item next to a light one. Most damage claims in fulfilment are created at the packing bench, not in transit.

  3. Pack rate held over time

    Units packed in a timed run, measured again later in the session. We report both numbers rather than the flattering one.

  4. Labelling and dispatch discipline

    Right label on the right parcel, and what he does when a label will not scan. A parcel with the wrong label is worse than a parcel that was never packed.

Papers and what we will not promise

Packing has no meaningful trade certificate and we will not pretend otherwise. You get the assessment record — accuracy counts, pack rate at two points in the session, handling observations — and the work history we could verify, with a plain note on what we could not.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about packing helpers

Can they use a scanner and a packing station system?

Tell us the system and we put the man through a scan-and-pack flow on a device instead of assuming. Some are quick with it and some are not, and you should know before he is standing at your bench.

How do you measure accuracy, exactly?

Orders packed against a list, with wrong, missing and extra items counted separately. We give you the numbers rather than the word “careful”, so you can compare men honestly.

We need them for peak season. How fast?

Preparation runs in parallel with your document attestation. Give us the date you need them working and we will tell you honestly whether it is achievable rather than agreeing and slipping later.

Do you supply women packers?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your contract needs female staff we will tell you that at the start rather than take the requirement and try to fill it.

Tell us how many packing helpers you need for the UAE

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to the UAE.
