Hire pipe fitters from India for the Gulf

Pipe fitters from India, each made to work from an isometric, set a root gap and align a spool at our New Delhi centre before his profile reaches you. A fitter who presents a joint square is the reason your welders weld once instead of twice. We supply fitters to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the fitting job, not just the trade

Fitters differ more than their job title suggests. These details decide who reaches your shortlist.

Spool shop or site erection

Shop fabrication and field erection are different experience, and the profile says which a man has.

Shutdown or routine

A turnaround window needs men who keep tolerance under time pressure.

Isometrics

Whether he must work from isos unaided — tested and reported separately from fit-up.

Fitter–welder ratio

How many welders each fitter must keep fed; the commonest reason a crew underperforms.

Indoor or open-air

Open-air tie-in and rack work runs into each country’s midday summer stop.

Remote or camp posting

Say so at interview; men who accept knowing the facts are the ones who stay.

Certificates and papers

ITI Fitter certificates and earlier refinery or fabrication records are sent with the profile where held, and listed for exactly what they cover; a facility pass from previous Gulf service is never presented as a general qualification. What employers ask about hiring pipe fitters from India

Can they work from isometrics without hand-holding?

That is exactly what the test checks, and the assessment reports it for each man.

Do you supply fitters for shutdown and turnaround crews?

Yes. Tell us the window and the scale; shutdown crews are selected and tested for working fast under pressure.

Can you supply fitters and welders as one crew?

Yes, and many employers prefer it. Tell us the ratio and we will say whether it will keep your welders working.

Do your fitters have Gulf refinery experience?

Some do, and it is stated per man with the type of facility. Where a man has never worked in a refinery, the profile says so.

Can our supervisor test them before we select?

Yes, at the New Delhi centre or by video, with the isometric and material prepared so you are judging real work.

Can you supply small numbers?

Yes. Requirements of a few men are normal, and there is no minimum headcount for proper testing.

Tell us the pipe fitters you need

Shop or site, shutdown or routine, numbers, country and window. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
