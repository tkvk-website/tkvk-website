Why light drivers are tested before shortlisting

A light driver in the UAE is rarely just a driver. He crosses emirates in a day, deals with your customer at the door, handles tolls and fines on your account, and represents your company while doing it. The cost of the wrong man is not the salary — it is the fines, the damage and the customer.

So the assessment covers the driving and the conduct around it, and the file states the licence class he actually holds rather than the one his agent claimed for him.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the driving assessment covers

  1. Road assessment in a light vehicle

    Saloon, pickup and panel van. Lane discipline at speed, merging, roundabout judgement and reversing loaded into a tight bay.

  2. Multi-stop delivery run

    A route with several drops, judged on sequencing, parking without blocking, handover and paperwork. This is the actual job for most UAE light drivers.

  3. Licence class verified, conversion stated

    Indian licence class and validity confirmed, with what your side must arrange for the UAE licence set out plainly. Conversion rules differ and change; we confirm the current position rather than working from an old note.

  4. Conduct and cost discipline

    Speed and toll discipline, fuel card honesty, and how he behaves at a customer door. Fines and damage cost employers more than wages do.

What employers ask about light drivers

Will his Indian licence convert to a UAE licence?

Conversion eligibility differs by licence class and by case, and the rules change. We verify exactly what he holds, tell you the current position as we confirm it, and set out what your side must arrange. We do not promise a conversion that is not ours to grant.

Can one driver cover more than one emirate?

Yes, and most do. We test route following and multi-stop sequencing rather than assuming a man who knows one city can work across seven.

Automatic or manual?

Tell us which. Most Indian commercial drivers learn on manual; if your fleet is automatic we say so in the briefing and test accordingly.

Do you supply van and pickup drivers as well as car drivers?

Yes, tested on the class you name. Panel van handling loaded is a different test from a saloon and we run it as one.

How do you handle fines and toll discipline?

It is part of the assessment and part of the pre-departure briefing. We cannot promise a clean record, but we can put forward men who understand that the account is yours.

Tell us what your the UAE fleet runs

Send the vehicle classes, the routes and the number of men. We will tell you how we would assess for it and where the licence stands in the UAE.
