Hire mall and retail support staff from India for the Gulf

Male stockroom, replenishment, goods-receiving, food-court and trolley staff from India for Gulf malls, hypermarkets and retail chains — the work behind the sale. Each is assessed on stock accuracy, receiving and conduct in a public building, and placed only into job titles open to expatriate workers. We supply retail support staff to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Mall and retail support staff for each Gulf country

Retail is indoor work. The differences are nationalisation rules, the size of the malls and the shape of the trading week.

Mall and retail support staff for Saudi ArabiaTypical work: Mall back-of-house, hypermarket replenishment, chain stockrooms, food courts, trolley and car-park teams, goods receiving.
Since 19 January 2026 sales occupations carry a 60 percent Saudi quota, so we supply support roles, not shop-floor sales.
Mall and retail support staff for the UAETypical work: Super-regional mall back-of-house, hypermarkets, fashion and electronics stockrooms, food courts, car-park teams, tenant logistics.
Malls run like small cities, with access rules and trading past midnight at weekends.
Mall and retail support staff for OmanTypical work: Muscat mall back-of-house, hypermarkets, Salalah seasonal retail, convenience chains, food courts, stockrooms.
The reserved-occupation list is long and changing; each title is checked against the list in force.
Mall and retail support staff for QatarTypical work: Destination mall back-of-house, hypermarkets, department store stockrooms, food courts, car-park teams, tenant logistics.
Malls are leisure destinations; the file is built to clear the Qatar Visa Centre.
Mall and retail support staff for KuwaitTypical work: Large mall back-of-house, hypermarkets, fashion and electronics stockrooms, food courts, trolley teams, goods receiving.
Heavy concentration of trade and Thursday-to-Saturday peaks.
Mall and retail support staff for BahrainTypical work: Mall back-of-house, supermarkets, chain stockrooms, food courts, car-park teams, goods receiving.
A small market with a sharp weekend surge from causeway visitors.

Papers and terms

Retail support has no trade certificate and none is invented. The profile carries the assessment, an honest grade of working English and verified earlier experience. Men are placed only where wage, hours, rest day, accommodation and shift pattern are written down before they sign. Passport and ECR status, the GAMCA/Wafid medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are timed so that none of them lapses while your visa is processed. Recruitment and deployment are carried out by Taj HR Services under Government of India licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025.

What employers ask about hiring mall and retail support staff from India

Can you supply shop-floor sales staff?

Not for Saudi Arabia, where sales occupations carry a 60 percent Saudi quota. Elsewhere we check the exact title against local rules first.

Do your men have mall experience?

Some have mall experience and some only shop experience. The profile says which.

Can they work night replenishment and Ramadan hours?

Tell us the roster and we assess and brief against it, in writing, before they leave India.

Can you supply for a store or mall opening?

Tell us the number and date. The schedule follows your visa quota and attestation, and we say if the date will not hold.

Do you supply women for retail?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only and says so at the start.

Can you supply small numbers?

Yes. Small, specific requirements are handled on the same terms as large ones.

Tell us the mall and retail support staff you need

Job titles, store type, numbers, country and start date. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
