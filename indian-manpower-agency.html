An Indian manpower agency for Gulf employers — and how to check any of them

TKVK and Taj HR Services supply trade-tested Indian workers to employers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. TKVK trains and tests the men; Taj HR Services, a Government of India licensed recruiting agent, recruits and deploys them. Below is what that gives you, and the eight checks worth running on any Indian manpower agency before you sign.

Eight checks before you sign with any Indian manpower agency

These apply to every agency, including us. Each card says what to ask, and how this route answers it.

1. A licence you can verify yourself

Ask for the recruiting agent licence number and look it up on eMigrate. Here: B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, Taj HR Services. How to verify it.

2. Who actually holds the licence

A brand, a testing centre and a licensed agent are not the same thing. Here the split is stated plainly: TKVK tests, Taj HR Services recruits and deploys.

3. Evidence before the interview

A CV and a certificate prove little. Ask what was tested and how. Here you receive the assessment and photographs of the work before any interview is booked, and you may run your own test.

4. A written quotation

Inclusions and exclusions listed separately, the basis of the fee stated, payment and refund terms in writing. A price given in conversation is not a price you can hold anyone to. Cost questions.

5. The worker does not pay for the job

Ask directly. Here the model is employer-funded, and India’s emigration rules cap what a recruiting agent may collect from a worker.

6. Replacement terms, defined

A replacement promise without a period and conditions is no promise. Taj HR Services publishes a 90-day replacement guarantee; the terms for your order are written into your agreement. Replacement questions.

7. Emigration clearance handled properly

Workers on ECR passports need Protector of Emigrants clearance through eMigrate before they fly. Only a licensed agent can file it, and it cannot be skipped. Visa and document questions.

8. An agency that will say no

A wrong yes costs you months. Here, if a requirement needs female staff, or a date depends on a permit you have not applied for, you are told at the start.

What employers ask about choosing an Indian manpower agency

Is TKVK a licensed manpower agency?

TKVK is a skill-training and trade-testing centre, not the licence holder. Recruitment, emigration clearance and deployment are carried out by its partner Taj HR Services, which holds Government of India recruiting agent licence B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by the Ministry of External Affairs.

How do I check an Indian manpower agency's licence?

On the Government of India eMigrate portal (emigrate.gov.in), in the Recruiting Agent section, search the registered list by licence number or agency name. For this route the number is B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025 and the name is Taj HR Services. Check it before you sign, not after.

Does the worker pay a fee to get the job?

No. The model is employer-funded: the worker is not charged for the job, and Taj HR Services publishes an Employer Pays Principle. India's emigration rules also cap what a recruiting agent may collect from a worker.

What happens if a worker does not perform?

Taj HR Services publishes a 90-day replacement guarantee for placed workers who do not meet the agreed standard. The terms that apply to your own order, including conditions and exclusions, are set out in writing before you commit.

Where is the agency based?

In New Delhi, at New Friends Colony, where the trade testing is done and employers can visit. A second training centre is in Lucknow. The work is for the six GCC countries only.
