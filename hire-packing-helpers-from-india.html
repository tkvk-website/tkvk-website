Hire packing helpers from India for the Gulf

Production packers, order packers and repackers from India, assessed at our New Delhi centre on pack rate at two points in a session, label and expiry-code accuracy, and hygiene as a habit. The second pack-rate reading is the useful one, and it is written down. We supply packing helpers to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.

Tell us the line and the product

Line packing and multi-item order packing are different skills. These details decide the shortlist.

Line or order packing

A single product on a line, or several items into one parcel for a customer.

Product

Food, beverages, seafood, FMCG, cosmetics or pharmacy.

Line speed

For automated lines, the speed he must keep up with.

Environment

Chilled, wet or cold-store work is harder than a dry warehouse.

Changeovers

How often the line switches product in a day.

Second duties

Weighing, labelling, loading or dispatch alongside packing.

What we check when packers are assessed

Pack rate held over timeUnits packed in a timed run, then again later. Plenty of men start fast and drop; we record who.
Labels, batch and expiryThe right label on the right product, and date codes read correctly — the error that turns into a recall.
Hygiene habitHand washing, gloves, hairnet, no jewellery, and what he does when something drops.
Counts and sealsCounting into a carton without recounting three times, and seals that survive a pallet and a truck.

What employers ask about hiring packing helpers from India

Can they work on a fast automated line?

Tell us the line speed and we assess against it. Hand-packing pace and feeding a machine are not the same.

Do they understand food safety?

Hygiene is covered and tested as habit. A real food-handling certificate is named; where there is none, the profile says so.

We need them for a seasonal peak. How fast?

Preparation runs alongside your attestation. Send the line date and you get a dated plan against it.

Can packers also load and dispatch?

Often, and it is a common combined role. Name the second duty and we assess it too.

Do you supply women packers?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only and says so at the start.

We only need a few packers. Is that worth doing?

Yes. Small lines feel a bad hire fastest, which is why testing matters.

Product, line type, numbers, country and the date you need them on the line. You will get a straight answer and our terms in writing.
