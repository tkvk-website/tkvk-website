Why heavy equipment operators are tested before shortlisting

With operators the risk is never slow work. It is a dropped load, a struck trench wall, a clipped scaffold, and a site that stops while an investigation runs. A man who is smooth on a 20-tonne excavator can be genuinely dangerous on a telehandler, and licence paperwork rarely shows the difference.

The UAE adds a second problem that agencies tend to keep quiet about. Operating licences here are issued locally after local testing — an Indian licence or training certificate is not a substitute, though a valid foreign licence in the same category can shorten the training hours required. So the real question for you is not only whether the man can operate, but how many weeks and how much cost sit between his arrival and his first legal shift.

We answer both. He is tested on the machine here so you know what you are getting, and we tell you what your side must arrange locally rather than letting you find out after he lands.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to the UAE runs through it.

What the operator test covers

  1. Machine handling under observation

    He operates the machine he claims, judged by someone who knows it — control, smoothness, load discipline and awareness of the swing radius.

  2. Pre-start checks

    Whether he walks the machine and checks it, or climbs on and turns the key. This one habit predicts most of what follows.

  3. Site awareness and signalling

    Banksman signals, spotters, exclusion zones, and whether he stops when he cannot see. The right answer is always that he stops.

  4. Licence category and honest gaps

    His Indian licence category and validity are verified and stated, and we say plainly which machines he is licensed for and which he has only operated.

  5. Heat and long-shift discipline

    Cab work through a UAE summer, the midday break rules on site, and recognising heat strain in himself before it becomes an incident.

Certificates and papers

Indian driving and operator licences are verified for category and validity and sent with the profile. We state clearly which machines a man is licensed for and which he has only operated — the two are not the same and we will not blur them. Alongside the trade papers, passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared for the UAE process. What we cannot do is issue you a UAE operating licence from India; that is arranged locally, and we tell you so before you commit.

What employers ask about heavy equipment operators

Will his Indian licence work in the UAE?

Not on its own. Operating licences are issued locally after local testing, and a valid Indian licence in the same category may reduce the training hours rather than remove the requirement. We prepare and verify his documents, and we tell you what your side must arrange.

Which machines do you test for?

Excavator, backhoe, wheel loader, telehandler and crane, subject to what we can genuinely test on. Tell us the machine and we will confirm before we take the order.

Do you supply certified crane operators?

Ask for the specific certification you need. Where a man does not hold it, we say so rather than implying he does. That is the whole point of asking us rather than a CV.

Can you send a maintenance mechanic with the operators?

Yes, and on a fleet of any size it is worth doing. Tell us the fleet and we will propose the mix.

Tell us how many operators you need

Numbers, salary and terms, and the date you need them on site. You will get a straight answer — including when the answer is no. You can also see all trades we supply to the UAE.
