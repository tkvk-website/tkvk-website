How the trade test works

We run the test the way your site would run it — a practical job, not a paper certificate. Nobody reaches your shortlist on the strength of a document alone.

  1. Your requirement sets the standard

    Tell us the trade, the work, the codes or standards on your project, and the tools your site uses. We test against that, not against a generic syllabus.

  2. Practical test in the trade

    The welder welds. The electrician wires and meggers. The operator operates. The driver drives. The work is judged on finish, method, speed and safety habits — the things a foreman notices in the first hour.

  3. Honest pass or fail, recorded

    A man who fails is told what to fix and retested later. He does not quietly get pushed into your shortlist. You receive the assessment with the profile, including where he is weak.

  4. You see the evidence before you interview

    Photographs or video of the test piece, the assessment sheet, trade certificates and experience papers — sent before the interview, so your technical man can screen without flying anywhere.

  5. Your own test, if you want one

    Many employers send their own technical representative, or run a video test. We arrange the workshop, the material and the candidates for it. You are never asked to take our word for it.

What we fix before he flies

Tools and method

Many men are skilled but trained on different equipment. We put them on the tools and consumables common on Gulf sites, so the first week is production, not orientation.

Safety discipline

PPE as habit, permit-to-work, height and confined-space basics, and stopping work when something is wrong. Site safety records depend on habits formed before arrival.

Site language

Enough working English and Arabic to take an instruction, read a sign, ask a question and report a problem. Not fluency — the vocabulary the job actually needs.

Documents in order

Passport and ECR status, GAMCA or Wafid medical, police clearance, certificate attestation, eMigrate PoE — sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

Expectations set straight

Salary, hours, overtime, accommodation and contract terms are explained in his own language before he signs. Workers who understood the terms do not walk off in month two.

Country rules

Each Gulf country clears workers differently. We prepare to the destination's requirement from day one rather than discovering a gap at the visa stage.

Six countries, and no others

We work for the Gulf only. Every country clears workers through its own system, and preparation differs accordingly. Each name below opens that country’s page.

  • Saudi Arabia KSA

    Hiring runs through the Musaned platform, with the contract registered before travel. Medical fitness from a GCC approved centre. Heaviest demand in construction, oil and gas, and facility services.

  • United Arab Emirates UAE

    The job offer is filed with MoHRE and signed by the worker before departure, so the terms must be settled early. Construction, hospitality, logistics and retail.

  • Oman

    Labour clearance and employment visa come from the Ministry of Labour against your approved requirement, so worker documents need to be ready in advance.

  • Qatar

    Medical, biometrics and contract signing are completed at a Qatar Visa Centre in India. Paperwork errors surface here rather than at your gate.

  • Kuwait

    Work permits are issued against your manpower quota, and educational or trade certificates generally require attestation. Attestation starts as soon as a file opens.

  • Bahrain

    Visas are issued through the Labour Market Regulatory Authority against a named employer. Steady demand in facility management, hospitality and light industry.

From your requirement to arrival

  1. You send the requirement

    Trade, number of men, salary and terms, accommodation and food, contract length, and the date you need them on site. A demand letter, power of attorney and job order are the formal documents; we will tell you exactly what your country needs.

  2. We source and test

    Candidates are drawn from our own trained pool and from sourcing across Indian states, then trade-tested against your standard. Only those who pass go forward.

  3. You shortlist and interview

    You receive profiles with test evidence. Interviews run in person at our centre or by video, at your convenience and your timezone.

  4. Visa, medical and clearance

    Medical at approved centres, police clearance, attestation, visa stamping, and eMigrate PoE clearance where it applies. Taj HR Services handles this as the licensed agent.

  5. Briefing, ticketing, arrival

    Pre-departure briefing on your contract and site rules, then travel arranged to your required date. You are told immediately if anything threatens that date.

What employers ask us

Are you a licensed recruiting agency?

TKVK is the training and trade-testing side. The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India's Ministry of External Affairs — and all deployment runs through it. You are dealing with one office and one accountable licence, not a chain of sub-agents.

Can we run our own trade test?

Yes, and we encourage it. Send your technical representative to our centre, or run it by video. We arrange the candidates, the workshop and the material. Our own test is there to save you time, not to replace your judgement.

How long from job order to men on site?

It depends on the country, the trade, and how quickly visas and attestation move — not on anything an agency can promise in advance. We give you a realistic range for your specific case at the start, and tell you the moment something slips rather than at the end.

Do you supply for one-off requirements or only bulk?

Both. Small technical requirements are often harder than bulk, because one wrong man is the whole order. Tell us the number and we will tell you honestly whether we can serve it well.

Who pays the recruitment cost?

Indian law caps what a worker may be charged, and several Gulf employers now insist on employer-pays recruitment as a matter of policy. Tell us which model you follow and we will put the cost breakdown in writing before anything starts.

What happens if a worker does not perform?

Ask us directly and we will give you our replacement terms in writing before you commit. We would rather state them clearly than leave you to assume.
