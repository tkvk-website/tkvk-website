What TKVK does for a worker

Most workers lose a Gulf job for reasons that have nothing to do with skill — a failed trade test, a medical that expired, an ECR passport without PoE clearance, or a contract they signed without reading. TKVK closes those gaps before they cost you a job.

Practice trade tests

Documents and PoE

Passport and ECR status, GAMCA or Wafid medical, police clearance, certificate attestation, and eMigrate PoE — checked in the right order so nothing expires while you wait.

Six countries, and no others

TKVK prepares workers for the Gulf only. We do not handle Europe, Canada, Malaysia or any other destination, and we will say so plainly if you ask. Every Gulf country runs its own hiring and clearance system, so preparation is not the same for all six.

  • Saudi Arabia KSA

    Hiring runs through the Musaned platform, and the work contract is registered there before you travel. Medical fitness must come from a GCC approved centre. The largest demand is in construction, oil and gas, and facility services.

  • United Arab Emirates UAE

    The employer files a job offer with MoHRE and you sign it before departure — the terms in that offer are what the contract must match. Read it and keep your copy. Strong demand in construction, hospitality, logistics and retail.

  • Oman

    The employer obtains labour clearance and an employment visa from the Ministry of Labour before your visa is issued. Medical and police clearance requirements are checked at that stage, so documents need to be ready early.

  • Qatar

    Most Indian workers complete medical tests, biometrics and contract signing at a Qatar Visa Centre in India before flying. That means your paperwork has to be correct here, not at the airport there.

  • Kuwait

    Work permits are issued against the employer's approved manpower quota, and educational or trade certificates usually need attestation. Attestation takes time, so it starts as soon as your file opens.

  • Bahrain

    Work visas are issued through the Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA), tied to a named employer. Smaller market than the others, with steady demand in facility management, hospitality and light industry.

One rule covers all six: they are all ECR notified countries, so if you hold an ECR passport you need PoE clearance on eMigrate before you can leave. Anyone telling you to skip that is putting you at risk.

How it works, start to boarding

  1. Come in and get assessed

    Bring your passport, trade certificates and any Gulf experience papers. We check your trade level honestly and tell you which countries and roles are realistic for you.

  2. Train and clear the trade test

    Close the gaps the assessment found, then take the practice test. You get a clear pass or fail, not a certificate for attending.

  3. Get your documents in order

    Passport and ECR status, medical at an approved centre, police clearance, attestation. We sequence these so the earliest one does not expire before the visa comes.

  4. Interview with the employer

    Your file goes to Taj HR Services for live mandates. Employer interviews happen in person or online, and you are told the salary, working hours, overtime, food and accommodation terms before you accept.

  5. Visa, PoE and departure

    Visa stamping, eMigrate PoE clearance where required, ticket, and a pre-departure briefing on your contract and your rights in that country.

Questions workers actually ask

Do I have to pay TKVK to get a Gulf job?

Recruitment charges to workers are capped by Indian law. What a service costs is told to you in writing before you pay, and every payment gets a receipt. If anyone asks you for cash without a receipt, that is not us — stop and call the office number on this page.

What is a trade test, and why does the employer insist on it?

A trade test is a practical test of your real skill — welding a joint, wiring a board, driving the vehicle. A certificate tells the employer what you studied; the trade test tells him how you work. We run the same test first, so you find out here instead of in front of the employer.

Which documents do I need for Gulf employment?

A valid passport with the correct ECR or ECNR status, a medical fitness certificate from a GAMCA or Wafid approved centre, police clearance where that country asks for it, and your trade or education certificates. If you hold an ECR passport and are going to a notified country, PoE clearance through the eMigrate portal is compulsory.

What is the difference between TKVK and Taj HR Services?

Both operate under the same Government of India recruitment licence, B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025. TKVK Services is the worker-facing side — training, trade testing, documents and pre-departure. Taj HR Services is the employer-facing side, holding the mandates from Gulf companies. Same office, same licence, two jobs.

Do you send workers to Europe, Canada or Malaysia?

No. TKVK works for the Gulf only — Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain. If someone offers you a Europe or Canada work visa in our name, it is not us.

How long does the whole process take?

It depends on your trade, the country, and how ready your documents are — not on anything we can promise in advance. We will give you a realistic range for your case at the first assessment, and tell you what is slowing it down at every stage.
