Why accommodation helpers are assessed before shortlisting

Accommodation work in Oman is usually a small team covering a lot of ground — common areas, waste, sometimes grounds and light maintenance, often across more than one building.

That breadth is the job, so a man who can only do one thing well is less useful than one who keeps a reasonable standard across all of it and reports what he cannot fix.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Oman runs through it.

What the preparation covers

  1. Cleaning method and chemical safety

    Dilution, colour coding and not mixing products. We note who arrived with the habit and who was taught it here.

  2. Mixed duties across a site

    Common areas, waste, and where the job includes it, grounds and light maintenance. Say what the real scope is and we assess all of it rather than the cleaning alone.

  3. Conduct around residents and staff

    Knocking, privacy, and courteous handling of complaints. In smaller compounds the same faces meet daily, and friction compounds quickly.

  4. Working unsupervised across buildings

    A task set across more than one area, with the man left to it. We record what was actually completed rather than what he reported.

Papers and what we will not promise

No trade certificate exists for this work and we will not manufacture one. You get the preparation record — method, chemicals, conduct and what he completed unsupervised — plus the work history we were able to verify.

Alongside the licence, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process.

What employers ask about building helpers

Can one man cover cleaning and grounds work?

Often, and it is the normal shape of the job here. Tell us grounds work is included and we assess it, rather than sending a cleaner who is surprised to be handed a rake.

Do you supply for locations outside Muscat?

Yes. Tell us the location and the living arrangement plainly and we brief the men on it before they fly. A man who did not know where he was going is a man who leaves.

How do you judge conduct?

By observation during preparation and by how a man handles being corrected. We report what we saw. We do not issue character certificates.

What documents do we have to provide?

Demand letter, power of attorney, job order and guarantee letter, attested in Oman and at the Indian mission. Send us the drafts before you attest — reading them takes an afternoon and a rejection costs weeks.
