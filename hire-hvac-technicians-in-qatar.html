Why AC and HVAC technicians are tested before shortlisting

Qatar's cooling estate is young and large, and it has moved from construction into maintenance. The venues, towers and districts built over the last decade now need servicing rather than installing, which shifts the demand from installers to diagnosticians — and those are not the same men.

District cooling carries a substantial share of the load in the newer districts, so plant-room competence is worth asking about explicitly rather than assuming it sits inside the words "HVAC technician". Chilled water, pumps, heat exchangers and BTU metering are a separate discipline from unitary service.

The paperwork is front-loaded here. Contract attestation and the Qatar Visa Centre medical and biometrics happen in India, so a file prepared carelessly at our end becomes a rejection there rather than a surprise you meet at the airport.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the trade test covers

  1. Fault-finding on a live unit

    A faulted unit and a systematic route to the cause. Method is what we grade.

  2. Central plant versus unitary

    Whether he has genuinely worked chilled water and plant rooms, or only split and package equipment. Stated plainly either way.

  3. Gas handling and pressure work

    Charging, recovery, leak detection and gauge reading, with clean refrigerant practice.

  4. Brazing and pipework

    Copper joint quality, insulation and support — the work that decides whether a system leaks in year two.

  5. Electrical and controls

    Contactors, capacitors, thermostats and control wiring, tested on the unit rather than claimed.

Refrigerants: what we actually check

One thing worth asking every candidate, and worth us testing: which refrigerants he has actually worked on. A man whose whole career was R-22 is not automatically safe on R-410A or R-32. The operating pressures are higher, the oil is different — POE rather than mineral — so contamination and moisture matter far more, and R-32 is an A2L, mildly flammable, which changes how he brazes, recovers and stores it. Gulf fleets are mixed: old R-22 units still running alongside newer R-410A and R-32 equipment, often in the same building. We record which refrigerants each man has genuine hands-on time with, and we say plainly where he has none, because "AC technician" on a CV tells you nothing about this.

Certificates and papers

ITI Refrigeration and Air Conditioning certificates and prior Gulf service records are verified and sent with the profile, with installation and maintenance experience stated separately. Passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are prepared alongside contract attestation so the file clears the Qatar Visa Centre first time rather than coming back.

What employers ask about AC and HVAC technicians

What actually fails at the Qatar Visa Centre?

The file, almost never the man — a name that does not match the passport, an expired medical, or an attestation nobody completed. We build the file for that checkpoint from day one and tell you if something on your side will not clear.

Do you supply maintenance men or installers?

Both, and we label them honestly. Qatar mostly needs the first now, and an installer sold to you as a service technician will show up as first-visit-fix failures within a month.

Do you supply women technicians?

No. TKVK supplies male workers only. If your requirement needs female staff we say so at the start rather than take the order and try to fill it.

Can you supply for a district cooling plant?

Ask for plant-room experience specifically. Only some of our men have it and we name which, rather than letting the trade title imply it.
