Why electricians are tested before shortlisting

Qatari work is inspected closely and consultants sign off on detail. Untidy termination or a man who cannot follow a schematic does not fail loudly — it fails at inspection, when the programme has no slack left. The practical test is how that gets caught before a visa is spent on him.

The recruitment licence is held by Taj HR Services — B-3252/DEL/PER/1000+/5/11251/2025, issued by India’s Ministry of External Affairs — and every deployment to Qatar runs through it.

What the electrical test covers

  1. Drawing to wiring

    A simple schematic, and the man wires it. Whether he reads it unaided is most of the answer.

  2. Termination and panel dressing

    Crimping, lugging, glanding and cable dressing. This is the single most common reason a new electrician is downgraded on site.

  3. Testing and measurement

    Continuity, insulation resistance with a megger, and correct multimeter use. A man who guesses instead of measuring is not an electrician.

  4. Isolation and safety

    Proving dead before work, lock-out discipline, and the confidence to refuse an unsafe instruction.

Certificates and papers

ITI Electrician certificates and wireman licences go with the profile where held, described for what they actually prove. A domestic wireman is never presented as an industrial electrician.

Alongside the trade papers, every candidate’s passport and ECR status, approved medical, police clearance and eMigrate PoE clearance are sequenced so nothing expires while your visa is in process, and the file is built so it clears the Qatar Visa Centre first time.

What employers ask about electricians

Do you distinguish domestic, commercial and industrial electricians?

Yes, and it matters. A man who has wired villas is not ready for a plant room. We test to the level your work needs.

Will their documents clear the Qatar Visa Centre?

That is what the file is built for. Medical, biometrics and contract signing all happen in India, so a careless file fails there — we prepare for that checkpoint from day one.

Do you supply electricians with HV experience?

Ask, and we will tell you what we genuinely have. High-voltage work is not something to overstate.

Can our engineer interview and test by video?

Yes. We arrange the candidates, the panel and the instruments, and your engineer runs it from Qatar.
