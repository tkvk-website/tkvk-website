Which workers we can supply — and which we cannot

Thirty-one questions about worker categories, each answered with a yes or a no rather than a maybe. Seven of the answers on this page are no, and they are the most useful ones on it.

Send your requirement All employer questions

Twenty-three tested categories, all male

TKVK tests and supplies twenty-three worker categories, listed in full on the trades page. Each man is graded on work he produced at the centre in New Delhi, not on a certificate, and the assessment goes to you before you interview him.

TKVK supplies male workers only. We do not recruit women for any category, which rules out a number of the requirements employers send us — and saying that here is cheaper for everyone than saying it after a demand letter has been attested.

Worker category questions

Skill levels

Which worker categories can you supply?

Twenty-three tested categories: welders, pipe fitters, electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, mechanics, masons, carpenters, steel fixers, scaffolders, forklift operators, heavy equipment operators, heavy and light drivers, delivery riders, security guards, cleaning and facility staff, household staff, hospitality staff, mall and retail staff, warehouse and logistics staff, packing helpers and residential building helpers. All male.

Can you supply skilled workers?

Yes, and skilled is the category the whole testing centre exists for. A man is called skilled here only after he has produced the work in front of an assessor, not because a certificate says he studied the trade.

Can you supply semi-skilled workers?

Yes. Semi-skilled men are tested on the same bench and graded honestly as semi-skilled rather than pushed up a band to win a requirement. Where you need a mixed gang, the assessment sheet tells you which man is which.

Can you supply general labourers?

Yes — packing helpers, residential building helpers and cleaning and facility staff are the general categories we test for. They are tested for fitness, discipline and reliability rather than trade skill, because that is what the job actually turns on.

Construction, mechanical and industrial trades

Can you provide welders?

Yes, and welders are the most tested trade in the centre. Every man welds a coupon that is inspected visually before he reaches your shortlist, and the assessment states the process and position he actually passed in.

Which welding processes do your welders know?

SMAW, GTAW/TIG and GMAW/MIG are the processes we test, in the positions the man claims. Send us your WPS or the process and position you need and we will test to it, then tell you which men passed and which did not.

Can you provide pipe fitters and fabricators?

Pipe fitters, yes, and they are tested on fit-up, alignment, gap and tolerance with a welder working beside them. Fabrication is close enough to this trade that we would rather see your drawing than guess — send it and we will say what we can cover.

Can you provide electricians?

Yes. The test covers conduit and cable work, terminations, DB work, continuity and insulation testing, and whether the man isolates before he works. The last of those is a safety question and it is scored.

Can you provide plumbers?

Yes. Pipe sizing, joints, fixture setting and pressure testing are all part of the test. Where a man is really a pipe fitter rather than a building plumber, the assessment says so instead of letting you discover it on site.

Can you provide HVAC technicians?

Yes — HVAC technicians are a tested category, covering split and package units, ducting, brazing, gas charging and basic fault finding. Where you need chiller or BMS experience specifically, say so, because that is a narrower pool.

Can you provide mechanics?

Yes. Tell us the machine family — light vehicle, heavy vehicle, plant or generator — because a good truck mechanic is not automatically a good plant mechanic and we will not present him as one.

Can you provide masons?

Yes, block and plaster masons both. The test measures line, level, joint consistency, plaster flatness and curing discipline, and output is measured together with quality because either one alone is misleading.

Can you provide carpenters?

Yes, and we insist on the distinction between shuttering and finishing carpenters. A good shuttering man can be a poor finisher, and the assessment states which kind each man is.

Can you provide steel fixers?

Yes. Bar bending schedules, cutting and bending accuracy, spacing, laps, tying and cover are all tested, because cover is the one error nobody can see after the pour.

Can you provide scaffolders?

Yes. Erection to a system, tie spacing, platforms and guard rails, and inspection and tagging discipline are tested. Whether a man reports a bad scaffold rather than patching it quietly is deliberately part of the assessment.

Operators and drivers

Can you provide crane operators?

Heavy equipment operators are a tested category; a crane operator licensed for a specific crane type is a narrower ask. Tell us the machine and the licence you need and we will say plainly whether we have men tested on it, rather than assume a machine is a machine.

Can you provide forklift operators?

Yes, forklift operators are a tested category. Operation, load handling, stacking discipline and pre-use checks are assessed, and prior licence or certification is listed for exactly what it covers.

Can you provide heavy-equipment operators?

Yes. Tell us the machine — excavator, loader, backhoe, roller, grader — because operators specialise and we report the machines each man has actually run rather than a general claim.

Can you provide light-vehicle and heavy-vehicle drivers?

Yes, both are tested categories, and so are delivery riders. Note that an Indian licence is not directly exchangeable in every GCC state — several require a local test — so plan for the licence conversion period rather than assuming day-one driving.

Security, facility, hospitality and logistics

Can you provide security guards?

Yes, with one important exception: security guard roles in Saudi Arabia are fully reserved for Saudi nationals, so we do not recruit guards for Saudi Arabia. For the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain we do, and each has its own licensing or ID requirement after arrival.

Can you provide cleaners and housekeeping staff?

Yes — cleaning and facility staff and household staff are both tested categories, and male only. We do not recruit women for any category, so if your requirement is for female housekeeping staff the answer is no.

Can you provide warehouse workers and loaders?

Yes. Warehouse and logistics staff and packing helpers are both tested categories, and forklift operators come from the same pool where you need the ticket as well as the hands.

Can you provide kitchen staff, waiters and hospitality workers?

Yes, hospitality staff is a tested category covering kitchen helpers, stewarding, service and housekeeping roles, male only. For a chef or a specific cuisine, tell us exactly what you need — that is a narrower pool and we will say so if we cannot fill it.

Can you provide facility-management workers?

Yes. Facility management requirements are usually a mix — cleaning and facility staff, electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians and security guards — and we will propose the mix against your contract scope rather than sending one category and calling it FM.

Categories we do not test or supply

Can you provide painters and insulation workers?

Not as tested categories today. Painting and thermal or acoustic insulation are not on the list of trades we test, and we will not present a mason or a carpenter as a painter. Send the requirement anyway and you will get a straight yes or no.

Can you provide riggers?

Rigging is not a separate tested category on our list today. Some scaffolders and steel fixers have done rigging work on site, and where that is true it is noted in the man's assessment — but we will not label him a certified rigger.

Can you provide supervisors and foremen?

We test tradesmen, not managers. Where a man has run a gang on site, that is recorded in his assessment and we will point him out to you, but we will not present him as a qualified supervisor or foreman on paper he does not hold.

Can you provide engineers and technicians?

Technicians in the trades listed above, yes. Degree-qualified engineers are not a category TKVK tests or supplies. If you need engineers, put the requirement to Taj HR Services directly and they will tell you whether they can serve it.

Can you provide HSE officers and safety staff?

No. HSE officers are a qualified, certificated role and not one we test. Safety behaviour is scored inside every trade test we run, but that is not the same thing as supplying a safety officer and we will not say otherwise.

Can you provide nurses, caregivers or healthcare staff?

No. Healthcare is a licensed profession with its own verification route, it is outside what TKVK tests, and most such requirements are for women — which we do not recruit at all. A clear no is more useful to you here than a maybe.

Can you provide IT, office or administrative staff?

No. TKVK is a trade testing centre for site and operational work. Office, administrative and IT roles are not tested here and we do not supply them.
